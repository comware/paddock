## Key Financial Assumptions

1. **No major crop failures** in first 2 years
2. **Consistent market access** (2-4 markets per week)
3. **Owner-operator model** (no paid labor initially)
4. **Gradual revenue growth** (30-50% year-over-year)
5. **No land purchase** in first 3 years
6. **Inflation** at 3% annually
7. **Interest rates** stable (if financing needed)

---

## Financial Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation | Cost |
|------|------------|------|
| Drought | Water tanks + efficient irrigation | $5,000-$10,000 |
| Crop failure | Diversification (10+ crops) | Built into plan |
| Market access | Apply to 5+ markets | Time investment |
| Cash flow gap | 6-month reserve | $15,000-$20,000 |
| Equipment failure | Quality purchases + maintenance | +10% on equipment |
