# Action Plan: Starting Your Small Farm in Victoria

**Date**: December 8, 2025
**Timeline**: 36 months to full-time operation
**Budget**: $100,000 AUD

---

## Immediate Actions (This Week)

### Administrative Setup
- [ ] Register Australian Business Number (ABN) at abr.gov.au (Free, same day)
- [ ] Choose business structure (Sole Trader recommended for starting)
- [ ] Open dedicated business bank account
- [ ] Set up basic accounting (spreadsheet or Wave/Xero)

### Market Research
- [ ] Visit 3-4 Melbourne farmers markets as a customer
- [ ] Note: products sold, pricing, presentation, customer traffic
- [ ] Talk to vendors about their experience
- [ ] Identify which markets have space for new vendors

### Resource Gathering
- [ ] Download Agriculture Victoria Farm Business Plan template
- [ ] Join VFMA mailing list (vfma.org.au)
- [ ] Follow Melbourne Farmers Markets on social media
- [ ] Research local microgreens suppliers for seed/equipment

## Phase 1: Home Microgreens Operation (Months 1-6)

### Month 1: Setup

**Week 1-2**:
- [ ] Order microgreens starter kit (seeds, trays, medium)
- [ ] Set up growing racks in your 50sqm space
- [ ] Install LED grow lights
- [ ] Start first test batches (sunflower, pea shoots, radish)

**Week 3-4**:
- [ ] Contact local council re: Streatrader registration
- [ ] Research public liability insurance options
- [ ] Create simple brand name and logo concept
- [ ] Set up Instagram/Facebook business pages

**Investment**: $2,000-$3,000
**Deliverable**: Growing system operational, first test harvest

### Month 2: Validation

- [ ] Harvest first microgreens crops
- [ ] Test sell to friends/family for feedback
- [ ] Calculate actual cost per tray
- [ ] Refine growing process based on results
- [ ] Complete Streatrader registration
- [ ] Obtain public liability insurance ($10M minimum)

**Investment**: $500-$1,000 (registration, insurance)
**Deliverable**: Legal to sell, validated growing process

### Month 3: First Sales

- [ ] Apply to 2-3 local farmers markets
- [ ] Purchase market stall equipment (marquee, tables, signage)
- [ ] Create professional packaging and labels
- [ ] Attend first farmers market
- [ ] Collect customer feedback
- [ ] Approach 1-2 local cafes with samples

**Investment**: $1,500-$2,500
**Deliverable**: First paying customers, market feedback

### Month 4-6: Optimization

- [ ] Attend markets weekly (minimum 2/week)
- [ ] Expand variety based on customer demand
- [ ] Build email list/social following
- [ ] Track all sales and expenses meticulously
- [ ] Optimize growing for highest-demand varieties
- [ ] Begin researching land lease options

**Monthly Revenue Target**: $800-$1,500
**Deliverable**: Proven market demand, growing customer base

## Phase 2: Land Lease & Expansion (Months 7-12)

### Month 7: Land Acquisition

- [ ] Visit 5+ potential properties in target regions
- [ ] Evaluate: water access, soil quality, infrastructure, proximity
- [ ] Negotiate lease terms (aim for 3+ year option)
- [ ] Secure land lease
- [ ] Assess water situation (existing bore, tank capacity needed)
- [ ] Get soil tests done

**Key Regions to Explore**:
1. Goulburn Valley (irrigated, fertile)
2. Peri-urban Melbourne (market access)
3. South Gippsland (good rainfall, if affordable)
4. Western District (most affordable)

**Investment**: $3,000-$5,000 (bond, first lease payment, soil tests)
**Deliverable**: Secured land with water access

### Month 8: Infrastructure

- [ ] Install first polytunnel (6x15m recommended)
- [ ] Set up drip irrigation system
- [ ] Install water tanks (20,000L minimum)
- [ ] Create tool storage area
- [ ] Prepare growing beds
- [ ] Order seeds and seedlings for first outdoor planting

**Investment**: $15,000-$25,000
**Deliverable**: Operational growing infrastructure

### Month 9: First Plantings

- [ ] Plant garlic (autumn planting for spring harvest)
- [ ] Start herb seedlings in polytunnel
- [ ] Plant first salad greens succession
- [ ] Establish cut flower beds
- [ ] Continue microgreens operation from home
- [ ] Add 1-2 new farmers market locations

**Investment**: $1,500-$3,000 (seeds, seedlings, supplies)
**Deliverable**: Diversified crop production begun

### Month 10-12: Growth

- [ ] Maintain succession planting schedules
- [ ] Begin restaurant outreach program
- [ ] Deliver samples to 10-15 local restaurants
- [ ] Secure first restaurant accounts
- [ ] Expand to 3-4 regular market days/week
- [ ] Consider used delivery vehicle purchase

**Investment**: $5,000-$15,000 (vehicle if needed)
**Monthly Revenue Target**: $3,000-$6,000
**Deliverable**: Multiple revenue streams operational

## Risk Checkpoints

### Red Flags to Watch

| Checkpoint | Warning Sign | Action |
|------------|--------------|--------|
| Month 3 | No market access secured | Apply to more markets, reassess |
| Month 6 | Revenue < $500/month | Evaluate product-market fit |
| Month 9 | Land not secured | Expand search, consider alternatives |
| Month 12 | No restaurant interest | Adjust approach, products, or target |
| Month 18 | Not profitable | Major strategy review |
| Month 24 | No path to full-time | Evaluate as side business |

### Go/No-Go Decision Points

**Month 6**: Should you invest in land?
- Go: Revenue growing, market demand proven, confident in skills
- No-Go: Can't sell product, no market access, skills gap too large

**Month 18**: Should you continue scaling?
- Go: Profitable or clear path to profit, enjoying the work
- No-Go: Consistent losses, burnout, better opportunities elsewhere

**Month 30**: Should you transition to full-time?
- Go: $50K+ profit, sustainable workload, passion for the work
- No-Go: Insufficient income, excessive stress, prefer side business
