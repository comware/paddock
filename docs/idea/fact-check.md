## Claims Requiring Local Verification

### Market Access

- [ ] **Farmers Market Availability**
  - Claim: Markets accepting new vendors
  - Action: Contact specific markets directly
  - Markets to contact:
    - Melbourne Farmers Markets (multiple locations)
    - VFMA accredited markets
    - Local council markets
  - **Why It Matters**: No point planning if you can't access markets

- [ ] **Restaurant Demand**
  - Claim: Strong farm-to-table demand in Melbourne
  - Action: Visit target restaurants, observe menus
  - Questions to ask chefs:
    - What do they struggle to source locally?
    - What would they buy from a new producer?
    - What price points work for them?
  - **Why It Matters**: Validates second revenue stream

### Land Availability

- [ ] **Lease Options in Target Regions**
  - Action: Check current listings on:
    - commercialrealestate.com.au
    - farmtender.com.au
    - gumtree.com.au
    - Local real estate agents
  - **Why It Matters**: Need actual options, not just theory

- [ ] **Water Access Status**
  - Action: Check with relevant water authority:
    - Goulburn-Murray Water (northern regions)
    - Southern Rural Water (southern regions)
  - Questions:
    - Are new allocations available?
    - What's the bore licensing situation?
    - Current allocation percentages?
  - **Why It Matters**: Water scarcity is real risk
