# PADDOCK

## Smart Farm Management for the Microgreens Revolution

---

### THE OPPORTUNITY

The Australian microgreens market is valued at **$21.3M USD** and growing at **8.6% CAGR**. Yet no software exists purpose-built for small-scale microgreens growers - a market of 50,000+ hobbyists and 5,000+ market gardeners in Australia alone.

---

### THE SOLUTION

**Paddock** is a privacy-first Progressive Web App that helps microgreens growers track experiments, optimize yields, and make data-driven decisions.

| Feature | Benefit |
|---------|---------|
| Tray Tracking | Monitor every tray from seed to harvest |
| 76 Growing Guides | Expert knowledge at your fingertips |
| Analytics Dashboard | Identify winning varieties |
| AI Assistant | Personalized growing advice |
| Offline-First | Works anywhere, installs on any device |
