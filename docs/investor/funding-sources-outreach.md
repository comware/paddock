## Detailed Source Profiles

### 1. Agriculture Victoria - On Farm Innovation Grants

**URL:** https://agriculture.vic.gov.au/grants

**Funding Range:** $10,000 - $150,000

**Eligibility:**
- Victorian-based primary producers or ag-tech businesses
- Projects that improve farm productivity, profitability, or sustainability
- Must co-contribute (typically 50%)

**Why Good Fit:**
- Paddock directly supports Victorian small-scale farmers
- Sustainability focus aligns with government priorities
- Local-first software reduces barriers for regional growers
- Strong alignment with "agri-food technology" priority area

**Application Deadlines:** Rolling applications, typically quarterly rounds

**Requirements:**
- ABN required
- Business plan summary
- Project proposal (innovation, outcomes, timeline)
- Financial projections
- Letters of support from growers

**Priority:** HIGH - Apply first

---

### 2. SproutX Accelerator

**URL:** https://sproutx.com.au/

**Funding:** $50,000 - $100,000 for 5-10% equity + 12-week program

**Eligibility:**
- Pre-seed to seed stage ag-tech startups
- Australian-based or willing to relocate
- Committed founding team

**Why Good Fit:**
- Australia's leading ag-tech accelerator
- Strong mentor network in agriculture and SaaS
- Access to grower networks for validation
- Direct path to follow-on investment
- Demo day exposure to ag-tech investors

**Application Period:** Typically Feb-March and Aug-Sept annually

**Requirements:**
- Working prototype (Paddock has complete MVP)
- Founder commitment to 12-week Melbourne program
- Pitch video
- Application form with traction metrics

**Priority:** HIGH - Excellent fit, apply in next cohort window

---

### 3. AgriFutures Australia

**URL:** https://www.agrifutures.com.au/

**Funding Range:** $15,000 - $500,000 (varies by program)

**Key Programs:**
- **evokeAG Innovation Challenge** - Up to $25K prizes
- **Emerging Industries Fund** - Larger grants for scale-up
- **Research partnerships** - Co-funded research projects

**Why Good Fit:**
- National ag-tech focus
- Microgreens is an "emerging industry"
- Innovation challenge is low-barrier entry point
- Research partnerships could fund guide/content development

**Deadlines:** evokeAG typically Nov-Dec, other programs rolling

**Requirements:**
- Innovation with measurable ag impact
- Clear pathway to adoption
- Australian focus

**Priority:** MEDIUM-HIGH - Apply to evokeAG Innovation Challenge

---

### 4. LaunchVic - Startup Funding Programs

**URL:** https://launchvic.org/

**Funding Range:** $20,000 - $100,000 (varies by program)

**Key Programs:**
- **Startup Development Program** - $20K-$50K for early stage
- **Founders of the Future** - Support for underrepresented founders
- **International Expansion** - Later stage

**Why Good Fit:**
- Victorian government agency supporting startups
- Ag-tech is a priority sector
- Founder-friendly terms (grants, not equity)
- Connections to Melbourne startup ecosystem

**Deadlines:** Rolling applications for most programs

**Requirements:**
- Victorian-based startup
- Clear innovation/technology component
- Growth potential and job creation metrics

**Priority:** MEDIUM - Good backup, apply after primary sources

---

### 5. CSIRO Kick-Start Program

**URL:** https://www.csiro.au/en/work-with-us/funding-programs/kick-start

**Funding:** Up to $50,000 matched funding (you contribute $10K-$50K)

**Eligibility:**
- Australian SME (revenue < $20M)
- Innovative R&D project
- Collaboration with CSIRO researchers

**Why Good Fit:**
- Access to CSIRO agricultural research expertise
- Could fund AI/ML model development for yield prediction
- Data science collaboration for analytics features
- Credibility boost from CSIRO partnership

**Deadlines:** Applications open year-round

**Requirements:**
- Matched funding contribution
- R&D component (AI/analytics work qualifies)
- Collaboration plan with CSIRO researcher

**Priority:** MEDIUM - Pursue if developing advanced analytics features

---

### 6. Angel Investors - Melbourne/Victoria

**Key Networks:**
- **Melbourne Angels** - https://melbourneangels.net/
- **Scale Investors** - https://scaleinvestors.com.au/
- **Startmate Angels** - Via Startmate network
- **Individual angels** in ag-tech (SproutX alumni network)

**Typical Investment:** $25,000 - $250,000

**Terms:** SAFE or equity, typically 10-20% for seed rounds

**Why Good Fit:**
- Faster decision cycle than grants
- Strategic value beyond capital
- Some angels have agriculture/food expertise
- Can lead to follow-on institutional investment

**Approach:**
1. Join Melbourne Angels pitch events (open to non-members)
2. Apply to Scale Investors female-founder program (if applicable)
3. Leverage SproutX network for warm introductions
4. Target angels with ag-tech portfolio companies

**Priority:** HIGH - Build angel pipeline in parallel with grants

---

### 7. NAB Agribusiness / Small Business Loans

**URL:** https://www.nab.com.au/business/industry/agribusiness

**Funding:** $20,000 - $250,000

**Terms:**
- Interest rates from ~7-12% (varies with security)
- Terms: 1-7 years
- Secured or unsecured options

**Why Consider:**
- Doesn't dilute equity
- Faster approval than grants
- NAB has dedicated agribusiness team
- Can fund specific equipment/marketing expenses

**When to Use:**
- Bridge funding while waiting for grant outcomes
- Specific capital expenditure needs
- If equity funding takes longer than expected

**Requirements:**
- Business plan
- Financial projections
- Personal guarantee may be required
- Ideally 6+ months trading history

**Priority:** LOW-MEDIUM - Reserve as backup option

---

### 8. Pozible Crowdfunding

**URL:** https://www.pozible.com/

**Funding:** Typically $10,000 - $100,000

**Model:** Rewards-based crowdfunding (no equity)

**Why Consider:**
- Pre-sales validate market demand
- No equity dilution
- Marketing benefit from campaign
- Engage early community of growers
- Australian platform with local audience

**Campaign Approach:**
- Offer early-bird lifetime subscriptions
- Physical rewards (microgreens growing kits)
- Behind-the-scenes founder access
- Named acknowledgment in app

**Timeline:** 30-60 day campaign, requires ~4 weeks prep

**Priority:** MEDIUM - Consider for Q3/Q4 if grant pipeline slow

---

### 9. R&D Tax Incentive (Australian Government)

**URL:** https://business.gov.au/grants-and-programs/rd-tax-incentive

**Funding:** Up to 43.5% refundable tax offset on eligible R&D

**Eligibility:**
- Australian company
- Conducting eligible R&D activities
- Turnover < $20M for 43.5% refundable offset

**Eligible Paddock R&D:**
- AI/ML model development for growing recommendations
- Novel analytics algorithms for yield prediction
- User experience research and experimentation
- Database architecture for local-first sync

**Annual Claim Potential:** $15,000 - $40,000 (on $35K-$100K R&D spend)

**Requirements:**
- Register activities with AusIndustry before FY end
- Document R&D activities contemporaneously
- File with tax return

**Priority:** HIGH - No application process, just claim correctly

---

### 10. Startmate Climate Track

**URL:** https://www.startmate.com/

**Funding:** $75,000 for 5.5% equity + 12-week program

**Focus:** Climate and sustainability startups

**Why Good Fit:**
- Sustainable agriculture is a climate category
- Local food production reduces transport emissions
- Strong mentor network
- Access to follow-on funding from Startmate portfolio

**Deadlines:** Typically 2 cohorts per year (March, September)

**Competition:** Highly competitive, ~1-3% acceptance rate

**Requirements:**
- Climate/sustainability focus
- Early-stage (pre-Series A)
- Full-time founder commitment

**Priority:** MEDIUM - Highly competitive but excellent if accepted
