| **Scattered knowledge** | Guides in books, videos, forums |
| **No performance analytics** | Can't optimize for profitability |

## Slide 3: The Solution

### Paddock: Your Digital Growing Assistant

**A local-first Progressive Web App (PWA) that helps microgreens growers:**

| Feature | Benefit |
|---------|---------|
| **Tray Tracking** | Monitor every tray from seed to harvest |
| **Growing Guides** | 76 variety-specific guides at your fingertips |
| **Analytics Dashboard** | Visualize patterns, identify winners |
| **Planting Calendar** | Plan succession planting effortlessly |
| **AI Growing Assistant** | Get personalized advice from Claude/GPT-4/Gemini |
| **Decision Scorecards** | Evaluate varieties with weighted criteria |
| **Time Tracking** | Know your true labor costs per variety |
| **Daily Logs** | Capture observations, environmental data |

**Privacy-First Design:**
- All data stored locally on your device
- Works offline - install as mobile app
- No backend, no subscription fees
- Your data is yours alone
