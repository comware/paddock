## Key Takeaways

1. **Microgreens are high-margin:** 60-80% gross margin per tray
2. **Quick payback:** Phase 1 investment pays back in under 3 months
3. **Scalable model:** Economics improve with volume
4. **SaaS compounds:** LTV:CAC of 8-24x enables growth investment
5. **Dual revenue streams:** Farm + SaaS diversifies risk
