## Overview

Lentil microgreens are an underappreciated variety with mild, pleasant flavor and good nutritional content. Green, brown, or French lentils all work well as microgreens, producing delicate shoots with small round cotyledons.

Lentils are extremely economical - perhaps the cheapest microgreen seed available. The mild flavor makes them versatile and accessible, though they lack the dramatic appeal of more flavorful varieties.

Growing lentils is straightforward, making them excellent for beginners learning technique with inexpensive seeds.

## Best Uses

- Mild salad component
- Smoothie addition
- Sandwich filler
- Budget-friendly microgreen mix component
- Learning/practice crop

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Green, brown, or French lentils
- Whole, undamaged seeds
- Food-grade lentils work fine

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- 2-3 years viability

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** Yes

**Process:**
1. Rinse thoroughly
2. Soak 8-12 hours
3. Drain completely
4. Seeds will swell noticeably

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Coco coir, soil, or hemp mat

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 150-200g per 10x20 tray

Spread in single layer, press gently into medium.

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 3-4 days

Standard protocol - cover, keep moist, 18-22°C.

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** When stems 3-4cm tall

Moderate light, 12-14 hours daily.

### 7. Watering

Bottom watering preferred. Keep consistently moist.

### 8. Harvest

**Timing:** 10-12 days, height 8-12cm, cotyledons green

**Yield:** 900g-1.5kg from 10x20 tray

### 9. Post-Harvest

Cut at soil level. Refrigerate, 5-7 days shelf life.

## Pro Tips

1. **Practice Crop** - Use lentils to learn technique. Cheap seeds mean low-cost failures.

2. **Grocery Store Seeds** - Food-grade lentils from grocery stores work fine and are very cheap.

3. **Mild Mixer** - Add to mixes where you need bulk without strong flavor.

4. **Economic Baseline** - Compare other varieties' economics against lentil baseline.
