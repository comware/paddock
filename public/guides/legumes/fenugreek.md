## Overview

Fenugreek microgreens offer one of the most distinctive flavors in the microgreen world - a unique maple syrup aroma combined with slight bitterness and curry-like notes. The flavor comes from sotolon, the same compound responsible for maple syrup's characteristic smell.

This distinctive profile makes fenugreek a specialty item. Some customers love it immediately; others find it unusual. It's particularly valued in Indian cuisine where fenugreek (methi) is a traditional ingredient.

The seeds are small and angular, releasing their distinctive aroma when soaked. Growing is straightforward but the specialized flavor limits market breadth.

## Best Uses

- Unique flavor profile for adventurous eaters

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Fenugreek seeds (methi)
- Small, hard, angular, yellow-brown seeds
- Available at Indian grocery stores cheaply

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- 2-3 years viability

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** Yes

**Process:**
1. Rinse seeds
2. Soak 8-12 hours
3. Distinctive maple aroma will emerge
4. Drain thoroughly

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Coco coir or soil

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 40-60g per 10x20 tray

Spread evenly, press gently into medium.

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 4-5 days

Keep at 18-22°C, mist daily.

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** When stems 3-4cm tall

Moderate light, 12-14 hours daily.

### 7. Watering

Keep moist but not waterlogged. Bottom watering preferred.

### 8. Harvest

**Timing:** 10-14 days, height 8-12cm, leaves developed

**Yield:** 200-400g from 10x20 tray

### 9. Post-Harvest

Distinctive aroma persists. Refrigerate, 5-7 days shelf life.

## Common Problems & Solutions

### Problem 1: Bitter Flavor

**Symptoms:** Microgreens taste too bitter

**Causes:** Grown too long, too much light, heat stress

**Solution:** Harvest earlier, moderate light

**Prevention:** Harvest at 10-12 days before bitterness increases

### Problem 2: Weak Aroma

**Symptoms:** Missing characteristic maple scent

**Causes:** Old seeds, insufficient development

**Solution:** Use fresh seeds, allow full development

**Prevention:** Fresh seeds, proper growing time
