## Overview

Mung beans are best known as the source of bean sprouts, but grown longer as microgreens they develop into substantial shoots with small leaves and a mild, fresh flavor. The familiar association with Asian cuisine makes them an easy sell to restaurants serving that market.

Mung bean microgreens are different from traditional mung sprouts - they're grown in soil or medium, develop green leaves, and have a more developed flavor than plain sprouts. This positions them between basic sprouts and more complex microgreens.

Very economical to grow with high yields and straightforward technique.

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Mung beans (green gram)
- Whole, green, undamaged
- Can use food-grade from Asian grocers

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- 2-3 years viability

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** Yes

**Process:**
1. Rinse well
2. Soak 8-12 hours
3. Drain and rinse
4. Seeds will swell significantly

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Coco coir, soil, or hemp mat

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 150-200g per 10x20 tray

Spread evenly in single layer, press gently.

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 4-5 days

Keep at 20-25°C (slightly warmer), mist daily.

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** When stems 4-5cm tall

Moderate light for leaf development.

### 7. Watering

Keep moist but not wet. Bottom watering preferred.

### 8. Harvest

**Timing:** 10-14 days, height 10-15cm, leaves developed

**Yield:** 900g-1.5kg from 10x20 tray

### 9. Post-Harvest

Cut at soil level. Use quickly - shorter shelf life than some varieties (4-6 days).

## Common Problems & Solutions

### Problem 1: Root Rot

**Symptoms:** Brown, mushy roots, smell

**Causes:** Overwatering, poor drainage

**Solution:** Improve drainage, reduce watering

**Prevention:** Don't overwater, ensure good drainage

### Problem 2: Mold

**Symptoms:** White fuzzy growth

**Causes:** Too wet, poor airflow

**Solution:** Improve ventilation

**Prevention:** Adequate spacing, air circulation

2. **Sprout Upgrade** - Position as premium upgrade from basic bean sprouts.
