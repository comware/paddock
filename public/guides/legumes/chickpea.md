# Chickpea (Garbanzo) Microgreens

## Quick Facts

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Difficulty** | Intermediate |
| **Days to Harvest** | 12-16 days |
| **Blackout Period** | 4-5 days |
| **Pre-soak Required** | Yes (12-24 hours) |
| **Seed Density** | 250-350g per 10x20 tray |
| **Expected Yield** | 4-6:1 ratio |
| **Flavor Profile** | Mild, nutty, chickpea-like with substantial texture |

## Overview

Chickpea microgreens produce sturdy, substantial shoots with a mild, nutty flavor reminiscent of the mature legume. The large seeds require thorough soaking and take longer to develop than smaller legumes.

The main challenge with chickpeas is their susceptibility to mold and rot due to the large, dense seeds holding moisture. Good drainage and airflow are essential. When grown successfully, they produce satisfying shoots that make excellent Mediterranean-inspired dishes.

Less commonly grown than peas or fava beans, chickpea microgreens offer differentiation in the market.

## Best Uses

- Mediterranean cuisine
- Middle Eastern restaurants
- Hummus accompaniment
- Substantial salad ingredient
- Unique menu item

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Chickpeas/garbanzo beans for sprouting
- Whole, undamaged, consistent size
- Desi (smaller) or Kabuli (larger) varieties both work

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- 2-3 years viability

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** Yes - critical for large seeds

**Process:**
1. Rinse thoroughly
2. Soak 12-24 hours (change water once if possible)
3. Seeds will double in size
4. Drain very thoroughly

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Well-draining coco coir or soil

**Critical:** Excellent drainage is essential

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 250-350g per 10x20 tray

Single layer only - never stack. Press gently.

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 4-5 days

**Key considerations:**
- Keep at 18-22°C
- Good airflow to prevent mold
- Check daily for rot
- Remove any moldy seeds immediately

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** When stems 4-5cm tall

Moderate light, 12-14 hours daily.

### 7. Watering

**Critical:** Don't overwater. Bottom watering only. Let surface dry slightly between waterings.

### 8. Harvest

**Timing:** 12-16 days, height 10-15cm, cotyledons developed

**Yield:** 1-2kg from 10x20 tray

### 9. Post-Harvest

Cut at soil level. Refrigerate immediately, 5-7 days shelf life.

## Common Problems & Solutions

### Problem 1: Mold/Rot

**Symptoms:** White fuzzy growth, slimy seeds, bad smell

**Causes:** Overwatering, poor drainage, poor airflow

**Solution:** Remove affected seeds immediately, improve conditions

**Prevention:** Don't overwater, ensure airflow, use fresh seeds

### Problem 2: Slow/Uneven Germination

**Symptoms:** Some seeds don't sprout

**Causes:** Insufficient soaking, old seeds

**Solution:** Longer soak, remove non-germinating seeds

**Prevention:** Full 24-hour soak, fresh seeds

---

## Pro Tips

1. **Drainage Critical** - More chickpeas fail from rot than any other cause. Drainage is everything.

2. **Lower Humidity** - Unlike most microgreens, chickpeas prefer drier conditions.

3. **Mediterranean Market** - Target Mediterranean, Middle Eastern, and Indian restaurants.

4. **Check Daily** - Inspect trays daily during blackout. One moldy seed can spread.

5. **Fresh Seeds** - Old chickpeas have poor germination and mold more easily.
