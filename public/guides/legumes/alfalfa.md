## Overview

Alfalfa is one of the most recognized sprouts in the world, commonly found in grocery stores. Grown as a microgreen in soil (rather than jar-sprouted), alfalfa develops more substance and nutrition than traditional sprouts while maintaining its signature mild, fresh flavor.

The appeal of alfalfa is its accessibility - the mild flavor offends no one, making it perfect for customers who don't want strong-tasting microgreens. It's also very economical to produce with reliable yields.

Soil-grown alfalfa microgreens are positioned as a premium upgrade to conventional sprouts.

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Alfalfa sprouting seeds
- Very small, uniform seeds
- Untreated (critical for edible use)

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- 2-3 years viability

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** Optional

Soak 4-8 hours if desired to speed germination.

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Fine coco coir or seed-starting mix

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 25-35g per 10x20 tray

Spread evenly using shaker. Don't cover.

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 3-4 days

Standard conditions - moist, 18-22°C.

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** When germination visible

Moderate light, 12-14 hours daily.

### 7. Watering

Gentle misting or bottom watering. Keep moist.

### 8. Harvest

**Timing:** 8-12 days, height 5-10cm, green and developed

**Yield:** 200-350g from 10x20 tray

### 9. Post-Harvest

Delicate - handle carefully. Refrigerate, 5-7 days shelf life.
