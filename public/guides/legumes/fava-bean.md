## Overview

Fava bean microgreens are large, substantial shoots with a sweet, nutty flavor reminiscent of the mature bean. The large seeds produce thick, sturdy stems and large cotyledons that provide satisfying texture and protein content.

Fava beans are one of the most economical microgreens to grow - seeds are inexpensive, yields are high, and the growing process is straightforward. The shoots are substantial enough to be a main ingredient rather than just a garnish.

The flavor is milder and sweeter than pea shoots, making fava beans accessible to a wide audience.

## Best Uses

- Stir-fry ingredient
- Sandwich filling
- Protein-rich addition to bowls

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Fava beans (broad beans) for sprouting/microgreens
- Large, flat seeds
- No splits or damage

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- 2-3 years viability

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** Yes - essential for large seeds

**Process:**
1. Rinse seeds thoroughly
2. Soak 8-12 hours in cool water
3. Seeds will double in size
4. Drain completely before planting

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Coco coir, soil, or can grow on hemp mat

**Setup:**
1. 2-3cm of medium
2. Pre-moisten well
3. Level surface

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 200-300g per 10x20 tray (heavy seeding)

**Technique:**
1. Spread soaked seeds in single layer
2. Seeds can touch but not overlap
3. Press gently into medium
4. Do not cover with soil

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 4-5 days

**Method:**
1. Cover with humidity dome or inverted tray
2. Keep at 18-22°C
3. Check daily, mist if needed
4. Watch for mold on damaged seeds

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** When stems 5-8cm tall

**Light requirements:**
- Moderate to bright light
- 12-14 hours per day
- Greening happens quickly

### 7. Watering

**Method:** Bottom watering ideal for large seeds

**Frequency:**
- Keep moist but not waterlogged
- Large seeds can hold moisture and rot
- Good drainage essential

### 8. Harvest

**Timing indicators:**
- 10-14 days from seeding
- Height: 10-15cm
- Cotyledons fully open, green
- Before first true leaves

**Yield:** 1.5-3kg from 10x20 tray (excellent yield)

### 9. Post-Harvest

**Handling:**
1. Cut at soil level
2. Substantial stems handle well
3. Rinse if needed

**Storage:**
- Refrigerate at 2-4°C
- Shelf life: 7-10 days
- Very good keeper

## Common Problems & Solutions

### Problem 1: Seed Rot

**Symptoms:** Mushy, smelly seeds

**Causes:** Poor drainage, overwatering, damaged seeds

**Solution:** Remove affected seeds, improve drainage

**Prevention:** Use undamaged seeds, don't overwater

### Problem 2: Mold on Seeds

**Symptoms:** White fuzzy growth on seed coats

**Causes:** Too wet, poor airflow

**Solution:** Improve ventilation, reduce moisture

**Prevention:** Adequate spacing, air circulation

### Problem 3: Slow/Uneven Germination

**Symptoms:** Some seeds don't sprout

**Causes:** Insufficient soaking, old seeds

**Solution:** Longer soak time, remove non-sprouters

**Prevention:** Full 12-hour soak, fresh seeds

## Pro Tips
