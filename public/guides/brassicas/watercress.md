## Overview

Watercress microgreens deliver the classic peppery, tangy watercress flavor in a form that's much easier to grow than traditional hydroponic watercress. The microgreen version doesn't require the water cultivation that mature watercress needs.

The flavor is distinctive - peppery with a bright, tangy note that's different from radish or arugula. Watercress microgreens are popular in British cuisine and among health-conscious consumers who know watercress's reputation as a superfood.

Seeds are very small and require careful handling. Growth is slower than radish but reliable once established.

## Best Uses

- Classic watercress sandwiches (British tea)
- Salads with peppery kick
- Soup garnish
- Steak topping
- Health-focused dishes (superfood reputation)

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- True watercress seeds (Nasturtium officinale)
- Very tiny seeds
- Don't confuse with upland cress or garden cress

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- Use within 1-2 years (viability drops faster)

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** No - seeds are very tiny

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Fine coco coir or seed-starting mix. Seeds need fine surface.

**Setup:**
1. 1-2cm fine, level medium
2. Pre-moisten well
3. Very smooth surface critical

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 10-15g per 10x20 tray (tiny seeds)

**Technique:**
1. Use shaker for even distribution
2. Mix with sand if needed for spreading
3. Do NOT cover - seeds need light
4. Mist very gently

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 3-4 days

Keep moist - watercress likes humidity. Cover loosely.

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** When germination visible

Moderate light, 12-14 hours. Keep consistently moist.

### 7. Watering

**Method:** Gentle misting or bottom watering

Keep moister than most microgreens - watercress likes moisture.

### 8. Harvest

**Timing:** 10-14 days, height 3-5cm (smaller than radish)

**Yield:** 60-100g from 10x20 tray (lower yield)

### 9. Post-Harvest

**Storage:** Refrigerate, 5-7 days shelf life. Keep slightly moist.

## Common Problems & Solutions

### Problem: Poor Germination

**Causes:** Old seeds, covered too deeply, insufficient moisture

**Solution:** Fresh seeds, surface sow, keep moist

### Problem: Weak/Leggy Growth

**Causes:** Too warm, insufficient light

**Solution:** Cool down, more light
