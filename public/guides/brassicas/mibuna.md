# Mibuna Microgreens

## Quick Facts

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Difficulty** | Beginner-Intermediate |
| **Days to Harvest** | 8-12 days |
| **Blackout Period** | 3-4 days |
| **Pre-soak Required** | No |
| **Seed Density** | 20-30g per 10x20 tray |
| **Expected Yield** | 7-9:1 ratio |
| **Flavor Profile** | Mild, slightly peppery, with narrow leaves |

## Overview

Mibuna is closely related to mizuna but with smooth, narrow leaves instead of mizuna's feathery, serrated ones. The flavor is similarly mild with a gentle peppery note - perhaps even milder than mizuna.

The narrow, elongated leaves provide a different visual element than the feathery mizuna, though both are Japanese mustard greens used similarly in cuisine.

Mibuna is less commonly grown than mizuna but offers variety for growers wanting to differentiate their Asian greens selection.

## Best Uses

- Japanese cuisine
- Salad mixes (texture variety)
- Light garnish
- Mesclun component
- Alternative to mizuna

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Mibuna seeds
- Small seeds, similar appearance to mizuna

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- 2-3 years viability

### 2-9. Growing Process

Very similar to mizuna - standard brassica protocol with gentle handling.

## Environmental Requirements

Same as mizuna - moderate temperature (16-22°C), moderate light, good humidity.

## Pro Tips

1. **Mizuna Alternative** - Offer alongside mizuna for variety.
2. **Smooth vs Feathery** - Market the difference in leaf shape.
3. **Same Care** - If you can grow mizuna, you can grow mibuna.
