## Overview

Green cabbage microgreens offer a milder version of the red cabbage experience - same easy growing, but without the dramatic purple color. The flavor is classic cabbage - mild, slightly sweet, and very versatile.

For those who want cabbage nutrition without the visual impact of red cabbage, green cabbage microgreens fit perfectly. They blend well in mixes and don't dominate dishes visually or flavor-wise.

Like all brassicas, green cabbage is rich in vitamins C and K, plus beneficial glucosinolates.

## Best Uses

- Salad bases and mixes
- Coleslaw style dishes
- Sandwiches and wraps
- Asian dishes
- Anywhere mild cabbage flavor is wanted

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Green cabbage variety
- Small, round seeds
- Similar appearance to other brassica seeds

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- 3+ years viability

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** No

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Any growing medium works well.

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 25-35g per 10x20 tray

Standard brassica technique - even distribution, light misting, no covering.

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 3-4 days

Cover, keep at 18-22°C, mist daily.

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** Cotyledons forming at 2-3cm

Moderate to bright light, 12-16 hours daily.

### 7. Watering

Bottom watering preferred, 1-2x daily. Avoid overwatering.

### 8. Harvest

**Timing:** 8-12 days, height 5-7cm, cotyledons fully developed.

**Yield:** 250-350g from 10x20 tray

### 9. Post-Harvest

**Storage:** Refrigerate at 2-4°C, shelf life 7-10 days.
