# Turnip Microgreens

## Quick Facts

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Difficulty** | Beginner-Intermediate |
| **Days to Harvest** | 7-10 days |
| **Blackout Period** | 2-3 days |
| **Pre-soak Required** | No |
| **Seed Density** | 25-35g per 10x20 tray |
| **Expected Yield** | 8-10:1 ratio |
| **Flavor Profile** | Mild peppery bite, hint of turnip sweetness |

## Overview

Turnip microgreens offer a mild peppery flavor with a subtle sweetness reminiscent of the root vegetable. They're similar to radish microgreens but generally milder, making them a good option for those who find radish too spicy.

Turnip seeds germinate quickly and grow vigorously, making them nearly as easy as radish. The microgreens develop nice color and texture with minimal effort.

Purple top turnip varieties can produce attractive purple-tinged stems for added visual interest.

## Best Uses

- Salads
- Sandwiches
- Mild alternative to radish
- Soups and stews
- Root vegetable dishes
