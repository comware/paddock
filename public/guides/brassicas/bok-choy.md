## Overview

Bok choy microgreens deliver the mild, slightly sweet flavor that makes mature bok choy popular in Asian cuisine. The microgreens have crisp white stems and green cotyledons, capturing the visual essence of the full-sized vegetable in miniature.

Growing is straightforward - bok choy is a reliable brassica that germinates quickly and grows steadily. It's one of the easier Asian greens to produce.

Bok choy microgreens are popular with Asian restaurants and health-conscious consumers familiar with mature bok choy.

## Best Uses

- Asian stir-fries (add at end)
- Soups and noodle dishes
- Fresh salads
- Dim sum garnish
- Any dish wanting mild Asian green flavor

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Bok choy (pak choi) seeds
- Shanghai or traditional varieties
- Small, dark seeds

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- 3+ years viability

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** No

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Coco coir or soil preferred for best stem development.

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 25-35g per 10x20 tray

Even distribution, light pressing, mist surface.

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 3-4 days

Cover, keep at 18-22°C, mist daily. Watch for white stem development.

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** White stems visible, cotyledons forming

Moderate light, 12-16 hours. Stems stay crisp and white with good airflow.

### 7. Watering

**Method:** Bottom watering preferred

Keep moist but ensure good drainage for crisp stems.

### 8. Harvest

**Timing indicators:**
- 8-12 days from seeding
- Height: 5-8cm
- White stems, green cotyledons
- Crisp texture

**Yield:** 250-350g from 10x20 tray

### 9. Post-Harvest

**Handling:**
1. Gentle handling to preserve crispness
2. Quick rinse if needed
3. Dry carefully

**Storage:**
- Refrigerate at 2-4°C
- Shelf life: 7-10 days
- Best when crisp - use while fresh

## Common Problems & Solutions

### Problem: Soft/Limp Stems

**Symptoms:** Stems not crisp

**Causes:** Overwatering, poor airflow, too warm

**Solution:** Improve drainage, increase airflow, cool down

**Prevention:** Good ventilation, moderate watering
