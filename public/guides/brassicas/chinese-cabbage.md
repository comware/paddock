# Chinese Cabbage (Napa) Microgreens

## Quick Facts

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Difficulty** | Beginner-Intermediate |
| **Days to Harvest** | 8-12 days |
| **Blackout Period** | 3-4 days |
| **Pre-soak Required** | No |
| **Seed Density** | 25-35g per 10x20 tray |
| **Expected Yield** | 8-10:1 ratio |
| **Flavor Profile** | Very mild, slightly sweet, delicate cabbage flavor |

## Overview

Chinese cabbage (Napa cabbage) microgreens are among the mildest of all brassica microgreens. The flavor is subtle and slightly sweet with minimal brassica bite - even milder than green cabbage microgreens.

The microgreens have pale green to yellow-green cotyledons with light-colored stems. They're popular in Asian cuisine and work well for customers who prefer very mild flavors.

Growing is straightforward with reliable germination and steady growth. Chinese cabbage prefers cooler conditions and can bolt if too warm.

## Best Uses

- Very mild salads
- Asian cuisine
- Dumpling and spring roll garnish
- Customers sensitive to strong flavors
- Blending in mild microgreen mixes

## Pro Tips

1. **Keep Cool** - Chinese cabbage prefers cooler conditions. Bolts in heat.
2. **Mild Option** - Market to customers who find other brassicas too strong.
3. **Asian Market Focus** - Popular with Asian restaurants and grocery.
