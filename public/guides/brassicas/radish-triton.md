# Radish (Triton/Purple) Microgreens

## Quick Facts

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Difficulty** | Beginner |
| **Days to Harvest** | 6-10 days |
| **Blackout Period** | 2-3 days |
| **Pre-soak Required** | No |
| **Seed Density** | 30-40g per 10x20 tray |
| **Expected Yield** | 8-10:1 ratio |
| **Flavor Profile** | Moderate spice with deep purple color |

## Overview

Triton purple radish (and similar purple radish varieties) offers deep purple coloring throughout the stems and even into the cotyledons. The spice level is typically moderate - more than Hong Vit but comparable to China Rose.

Purple radish varieties are popular for their dramatic visual impact. The purple color often extends higher up the stems than pink varieties, creating a more uniformly colored product.

Growing is identical to other radish varieties - fast, reliable, and forgiving.

## Best Uses

- Maximum color impact
- Dramatic garnish
- Salads needing color
- Photography
- Spice lovers wanting color too

---

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

Same protocol as all radish varieties. Very straightforward growing.

---

## Environmental Requirements

Same as other radishes. Bright light essential for deep purple color.

---

## Pro Tips

1. **Deepest Purple** - Triton and similar varieties produce the deepest purple color.
2. **Light is Critical** - Without bright light, purple becomes muddy.
3. **Spice + Color** - For customers who want both intensity and visual impact.
4. **Compare Varieties** - Try Hong Vit, China Rose, and Triton side by side to see differences.
