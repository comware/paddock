# Equipment Guide

Everything you need to start growing microgreens, from budget basics to professional upgrades.

---

## Essential Starter Kit

These are the must-haves to grow your first successful tray.

### 1. Growing Trays (with drainage holes)

**What:** Standard 10x20 inch (25x50cm) nursery trays with perforated bottoms.

**Quantity:** Start with 5-10 trays

**What to look for:**
- Sturdy plastic that won't warp
- Even drainage hole distribution
- Stackable design

**Cost:** $2-4 each

---

### 2. Solid Trays (no holes)

**What:** Same size trays without drainage holes, used for bottom watering.

**Quantity:** Half as many as growing trays (trays nest inside these)

**What to look for:**
- Rigid sides that won't bow under water weight
- Smooth interior for easy cleaning

**Cost:** $2-4 each

---

### 3. Growing Medium

**What:** The material seeds grow in.

**Best starter option:** Coco coir bricks or bags

**Why coco coir:**
- Naturally sterile (reduces mold risk)
- Excellent moisture retention
- Sustainable and affordable
- Easy to prepare and use

**How much:** One compressed brick makes ~8-10L, enough for 10+ trays

**Cost:** $10-15 for 10L equivalent

**Alternative:** Seed-starting potting mix (avoid fertilized mixes)

---

### 4. Seeds

**What:** Untreated seeds suitable for microgreen growing.

**Starter varieties (beginner-friendly):**
- Sunflower (large, easy to handle, forgiving)
- Pea shoots (reliable, delicious)
- Radish (fastest results, 5-7 days)

**Where to buy:** Look for "microgreen seeds" from reputable suppliers. Avoid birdseed or treated garden seeds.

**Quantity:** 500g-1kg per variety to start

**Cost:** $15-30 per kg depending on variety

---

### 5. Spray Bottle

**What:** Fine-mist spray bottle for watering during germination.

**What to look for:**
- Fine, even mist (not streams)
- Comfortable trigger
- 500ml-1L capacity

**Cost:** $5-15

---

### 6. Weights for Blackout

**What:** 1-2kg weight to place on top during blackout phase.

**Options:**
- Books
- Bricks wrapped in plastic
- Water-filled containers
- Another tray filled with water

**Cost:** Free (use household items)

---

### 7. Light Source

**What:** Natural or artificial light for the growing phase.

**Options:**

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|--------|------|------|
| Sunny window | Free, natural | Seasonal, direction-dependent |
| LED shop lights | Affordable, effective | May need mounting |
| Full-spectrum grow lights | Optimal growth | Higher cost |

**Minimum:** Bright window with 4-6 hours direct or strong indirect light

**Better:** LED light panel, 12-16 hours/day via timer

**Cost:** Free (window) to $30-100 (LED panels)

---

### 8. Sharp Scissors or Knife

**What:** Clean cutting tool for harvest.

**What to look for:**
- Sharp blade for clean cuts
- Comfortable grip
- Easy to clean

**Cost:** $5-20

---

### 9. Kitchen Scale

**What:** For measuring seed quantities consistently.

**What to look for:**
- Measures in grams
- At least 500g capacity
- Tare function

**Cost:** $10-20

## Upgrade Equipment

Once you're comfortable with the basics, these upgrades improve consistency and scale.

### LED Grow Lights

**Why upgrade:** Consistent light regardless of season or window direction.

**What to look for:**
- Full spectrum (includes red and blue wavelengths)
- Covers your growing area evenly
- Timer-compatible or built-in timer

**Setup:**
- Position 15-30cm above trays
- 12-16 hours on, 8-12 hours off
- Use outlet timer for automation

---

### Shelving/Rack System

**Why upgrade:** Grow more trays in less floor space.

**What to look for:**
- Wire shelving (airflow)
- Adjustable shelf heights
- Sturdy construction
- Fits standard 10x20 trays

**Typical setup:** 4-tier rack holds 8-16 trays depending on light setup

---

### Small Fan

**Why upgrade:** Air circulation prevents mold and strengthens stems.

**What to look for:**
- Small desk fan or clip fan
- Oscillating preferred
- Quiet operation

**Usage:** Low setting, gentle breeze across trays (not directly blasting them)

---

### Humidity Dome

**Why upgrade:** Maintains humidity during germination, especially in dry environments.

**What to look for:**
- Fits 10x20 trays
- Vented for airflow control
- Clear for visibility

**When to use:** During blackout phase for varieties sensitive to drying

---

### Precision Scale

**Why upgrade:** Measure tiny seeds (basil, amaranth) accurately.

**What to look for:**
- 0.1g precision
- At least 200g capacity
- Calibration function

---

### Thermometer/Hygrometer

**Why upgrade:** Monitor conditions for optimal growth.

**What to look for:**
- Displays temperature and humidity
- Min/max memory
- Wireless options available

**Optimal ranges:**
- Temperature: 18-24°C (65-75°F)
- Humidity: 40-60%

---

### Microgreen Harvesting Knife

**Why upgrade:** Faster, cleaner harvests for larger batches.

**What to look for:**
- Wide, sharp blade
- Comfortable handle
- Stainless steel for easy cleaning

---

## Equipment Care

### Cleaning Trays

**After each use:**
1. Remove growing medium (compost it)
2. Rinse with water
3. Scrub with brush if needed
4. Sanitize with diluted hydrogen peroxide (3%)
5. Air dry completely before reuse

### Storing Seeds

**For longest viability:**
- Cool, dry location (15-20°C)
- Airtight container
- Away from direct light
- Label with purchase date
- Most seeds last 2-3 years properly stored
