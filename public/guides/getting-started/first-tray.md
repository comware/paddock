# Your First Tray

A step-by-step guide to growing your first successful microgreens.

---

## Recommended First Variety: Sunflower

**Why sunflower is perfect for beginners:**
- Large seeds are easy to handle and count
- Germination is fast and visible
- Very forgiving of minor mistakes
- Delicious, satisfying results
- High yield to reward your effort

If sunflower isn't available, **pea shoots** or **radish** are also excellent first choices.

---

## What You'll Need

- [ ] 80-100g sunflower seeds (black oil variety)
- [ ] 1 growing tray (with holes)
- [ ] 1 solid tray (for bottom watering)
- [ ] Coco coir or growing medium
- [ ] Spray bottle with water
- [ ] Bowl for soaking seeds
- [ ] Weight (1-2kg - books, bricks, etc.)
- [ ] Dark space (closet, cabinet)
- [ ] Sunny spot or grow light
- [ ] Sharp scissors

---

## Day-by-Day Timeline

### Day 0 (Evening) - Soak Your Seeds

**Time needed:** 5 minutes + overnight soak

1. **Measure seeds:** 80-100g for a 10x20 tray
2. **Place in bowl:** Large enough for seeds to spread
3. **Add water:** Cover with 3x the volume of water (room temperature)
4. **Leave overnight:** 8-12 hours

**What to expect:** Seeds will absorb water and swell noticeably. Some may float - skim these off (they're often empty).

**Tip:** Set a reminder for tomorrow morning.

---

### Day 1 (Morning) - Plant Your Seeds

**Time needed:** 15-20 minutes

#### Step 1: Prepare the tray

1. Add coco coir to growing tray
2. Fill to 2-3cm depth (about 1 inch)
3. Level the surface gently (don't compact)
4. Spray until evenly moist (not soaking wet)

#### Step 2: Drain and rinse seeds

1. Pour off soaking water
2. Rinse seeds with fresh water
3. Drain well (no standing water)

#### Step 3: Spread seeds

1. Pour seeds onto prepared tray
2. Use fingers to spread evenly across entire surface
3. Seeds should touch but not pile on top of each other
4. Gently press seeds into medium for good contact
5. Mist the surface lightly

**Important:** Do NOT cover seeds with more medium. They sit on top.

#### Step 4: Set up blackout

1. Place another tray on top (inverted or right-side up)
2. Add weight: 1-2kg (books work great)
3. Move to dark location (closet, cabinet, under shelf)

**Temperature:** Room temperature is fine (18-24°C / 65-75°F)

---

### Days 2-4 - Blackout Phase

**Time needed:** 2 minutes daily

**Daily check:**
1. Lift the cover tray
2. Check if surface is drying out
3. Mist lightly if needed
4. Replace cover and weight

**What you'll see:**
- **Day 1-2:** Seeds swelling, small white roots emerging
- **Day 2-3:** Pale/yellow shoots pushing upward
- **Day 3-4:** Shoots 3-5cm tall, pushing against cover tray

**Ready for light when:**
- Shoots are 3-5cm (1-2 inches) tall
- They're pushing hard against the weight
- Most seeds have sprouted

---

### Days 5-10 - Light Phase

**Time needed:** 5 minutes daily

#### Step 1: Move to light

1. Remove cover tray and weight
2. Place growing tray inside solid tray
3. Position in bright location or under grow light
4. Shoots will be pale/yellow - this is normal!

#### Step 2: Daily care

**Watering (bottom water method):**
1. Add 1-2cm water to solid tray
2. Wait 15-30 minutes for medium to absorb
3. Drain any excess water
4. Repeat once or twice daily as needed

**Light:**
- Natural: 4-6 hours direct or bright indirect
- Artificial: 12-16 hours under grow light

**What you'll see:**
- **Day 1 in light:** Shoots start greening within hours
- **Day 2-3:** Vibrant green, cotyledons spreading open
- **Day 3-7:** Rapid growth, seed hulls falling off

**Tip:** Rotate tray daily if light comes from one direction.

---

### Days 10-14 - Harvest Time!

**Time needed:** 10-15 minutes

#### When to harvest

Your sunflower microgreens are ready when:
- Height: 10-15cm (4-6 inches)
- Cotyledons (seed leaves) fully open and green
- Most seed hulls have fallen off
- True leaves may be just starting (optional)

**Best time:** Morning, when greens are most hydrated

#### How to harvest

1. **Prepare:** Have a clean container ready
2. **Cut:** Use sharp scissors just above the soil line
3. **Work in sections:** Or harvest entire tray at once
4. **Remove hulls:** Pick off any remaining seed coats
5. **Rinse (optional):** Only if debris is present
6. **Dry:** Shake gently or use salad spinner

#### Storage

- Line a container with paper towel
- Place greens loosely inside
- Store in refrigerator (2-4°C)
- Use within 7-10 days for best quality

## Troubleshooting Your First Tray

### Nothing sprouted after 3 days
- Seeds may be old - try a new batch
- Temperature too cold - move to warmer spot
- Too dry - keep consistently moist

### Mold appearing
- Too wet - reduce watering
- Poor airflow - add a small fan
- Seeds too dense - use less next time

### Leggy, weak stems
- Not enough weight during blackout - use 1-2kg
- Light too far away - move closer
- Too warm during growing

### Seeds won't shed hulls
- Try misting heavily and brushing gently
- Pick off by hand for clean harvest
- Some hulls fall off naturally as greens dry

## Record Your First Grow

Start tracking in Paddock:

1. **Add a new tray** with today's date
2. **Log daily observations** (what you see, any issues)
3. **Record your harvest** weight when done
4. **Calculate your yield ratio:** Harvest weight ÷ seed weight

This data helps you improve with each grow!

## What's Next?

Congratulations on your first successful tray!

**Try these next:**
- **Radish** - Faster (5-7 days), spicy flavor
- **Pea shoots** - Similar process, sweet taste
- **Broccoli** - Small seeds, different technique

**Improve your setup:**
- Add a grow light for consistency
- Get a small fan for airflow
- Scale up with shelving

**Track and learn:**
- Use Paddock to log every tray
- Compare varieties and techniques
- Build your knowledge over time
