## Overview

Red cabbage microgreens are grown primarily for their stunning visual appeal. The deep purple to magenta stems and green-purple cotyledons create dramatic color contrast in any dish. The color comes from anthocyanins - powerful antioxidants also found in blueberries and red wine.

The flavor is mild cabbage - less pungent than mature red cabbage, with a pleasant, slightly sweet taste. The color remains vibrant even after harvest, making these microgreens a favorite for garnishing and plating.

Red cabbage microgreens are nutritionally dense, containing vitamins C and K, plus the beneficial anthocyanins that give them their color.

## Best Uses

- Garnish (primary use - stunning color)
- Salad mixes for color contrast
- Tacos and slaws
- Plating for upscale restaurants
- Photography and food styling

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Red cabbage variety specifically
- Small, round, dark brown/black seeds
- Organic preferred for eating raw

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- Sealed container
- 3+ years viability

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** No

Small seeds germinate well without soaking.

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Any - coco coir, soil, or mats all work well.

**Setup:** 1-2cm moistened medium, level surface.

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 25-35g per 10x20 tray

**Technique:**
1. Even distribution with shaker
2. Seeds close but not piled
3. Light misting
4. No covering with medium

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 3-4 days

**Method:**
1. Cover with inverted tray
2. Light weight (1-2kg) helps push off seed hulls
3. Keep at 18-22°C
4. Mist daily

**What to expect:**
- Day 1-2: Germination begins
- Day 2-3: Pale/pink stems emerge
- Day 3-4: Purple color developing, ready for light

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** Stems 2-3cm with cotyledons forming

**Light requirements:**
- Bright light essential for best color
- 12-16 hours per day
- More light = deeper purple color

**What happens:**
- Dramatic color development
- Purple intensifies over days
- Cotyledons turn green with purple edges

### 7. Watering

**Method:** Bottom watering preferred

**Frequency:**
- 1-2x daily in light phase
- Keep moist but not wet
- Brassicas are damping-off prone

### 8. Harvest

**Timing indicators:**
- 8-12 days from seeding
- Height: 5-8cm
- Maximum color development
- Before true leaves emerge

**Technique:**
1. Cut just above medium
2. Morning harvest for best appearance
3. Handle gently to preserve presentation

**Yield:** 200-350g from 10x20 tray

### 9. Post-Harvest

**Handling:**
1. Minimal washing - water dulls color slightly
2. Dry gently
3. Handle with care for presentation quality

**Storage:**
- Refrigerate at 2-4°C
- Shelf life: 7-10 days
- Color holds well when refrigerated

## Common Problems & Solutions

### Problem 1: Pale/Weak Color

**Symptoms:** Purple not vibrant, more pink than magenta

**Causes:** Insufficient light, too warm

**Solution:** Move closer to lights, increase duration, cool down

**Prevention:** Bright light from day 1 of light phase, cooler temps

### Problem 2: Damping Off

**Symptoms:** Seedlings collapse at soil level

**Causes:** Overwatering, poor drainage

**Solution:** Improve airflow, reduce watering

**Prevention:** Proper drainage, don't overwater, good ventilation

### Problem 3: Color Fading After Harvest

**Symptoms:** Purple color dulls quickly

**Causes:** Heat exposure, too much washing, age

**Solution:** Refrigerate immediately, minimal washing

**Prevention:** Quick cooling after harvest, dry storage

## Pro Tips

1. **Light = Color** - The single most important factor for vibrant purple is bright light. Don't skimp on lighting.

2. **Cool Intensifies** - Cooler growing temps (16-18°C) often produce deeper purple coloration.

3. **Presentation Star** - Market these specifically for garnish and presentation. Restaurants pay premium.

4. **Color Contrast** - Pair with bright green microgreens (pea shoots, sunflower) for maximum visual impact.

5. **Quick to Market** - Use within days of harvest for best color. Fresh sells better than stored.

## Scheduling Recommendations

- **Continuous supply:** Plant every 4-5 days
- **Match to orders** - Best sold fresh, time planting to demand
- **Year-round crop** - Grows well in any season with proper lighting

## Companion Planting

Grows well with:
- **Broccoli** - Same timing, complementary green color
- **Kale** - Similar requirements
- **Don't mix with radish** - Different harvest times
