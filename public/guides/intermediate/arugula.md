## Overview

Arugula (also called rocket or roquette) microgreens deliver the characteristic peppery, slightly nutty flavor of mature arugula in a more tender form. The microgreens have a milder bite than full-grown arugula while maintaining that distinctive taste that's popular in Mediterranean and Italian cuisine.

The leaves are small and elongated with a distinctive serrated or lobed shape even at the microgreen stage. The flavor develops more intensity with age - younger greens are milder, more mature ones are spicier.

Arugula is a cool-season crop that performs best in moderate temperatures. It can bolt (become bitter) if grown too warm.

## Best Uses

- Pizza topping (classic use)
- Salads - Italian and Mediterranean
- Pasta garnish
- Sandwich green
- Upscale burger topping

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Common arugula or wild arugula (sylvetta)
- Wild arugula has more intense flavor but slower growing
- Tiny, round, tan/brown seeds

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- Sealed container
- 2-3 years viability

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** No - very small seeds germinate well dry.

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Fine coco coir or soil works best. Seeds are tiny.

**Setup:**
1. 1-2cm fine, level medium
2. Pre-moisten thoroughly
3. Very smooth surface important for small seeds

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 20-30g per 10x20 tray (seeds are tiny, less weight needed)

**Technique:**
1. Use shaker for even distribution
2. Seeds should be close but not piled
3. Do NOT cover - seeds need light to germinate
4. Mist very gently - don't displace seeds

**Note:** Arugula seeds are tiny and can clump. Mix with fine sand for easier spreading.

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 2-3 days (shorter than many brassicas)

**Method:**
1. Cover but don't use heavy weight - seeds are delicate
2. Keep at 15-20°C (cooler than most)
3. Mist carefully daily

**What to expect:**
- Day 1: Seeds swell
- Day 2: White roots visible, stems emerging
- Day 3: Ready for light

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** As soon as stems are visible (day 2-3)

**Light requirements:**
- Moderate light is sufficient
- 12-14 hours per day
- Avoid intense heat from lights

**What happens:**
- Cotyledons open and green
- Characteristic leaf shape develops
- Steady but not explosive growth

### 7. Watering

**Method:** Bottom watering or very gentle misting

**Frequency:**
- Once or twice daily
- Keep moist but not wet
- Arugula is sensitive to overwatering

**Critical:** Arugula dislikes waterlogged conditions. Good drainage essential.

### 8. Harvest

**Timing indicators:**
- 8-12 days from seeding
- Height: 4-6cm
- First true leaves just starting to show (optional)
- Harvest earlier for milder flavor, later for more peppery

**Technique:**
1. Cut just above medium
2. Harvest in morning
3. True leaves can be included for more complex flavor

**Yield:** 150-250g from 10x20 tray

### 9. Post-Harvest

**Handling:**
1. Gentle handling - leaves are delicate
2. Light washing
3. Dry carefully

**Storage:**
- Refrigerate at 2-4°C
- Shelf life: 5-7 days (shorter than radish/broccoli)
- Best used fresh

## Common Problems & Solutions

### Problem 1: Bitter/Too Spicy Flavor

**Symptoms:** Overly peppery, unpleasant bitter notes

**Causes:** Heat stress, grown too long, water stress

**Solution:** Harvest earlier, grow cooler

**Prevention:** Keep cool (15-18°C), harvest at peak size

### Problem 2: Bolting/Stretching

**Symptoms:** Very elongated stems, premature flowering tendency

**Causes:** Too warm, insufficient light

**Solution:** Cool down, increase light

**Prevention:** Grow in coolest area, adequate lighting

### Problem 3: Clumpy Germination

**Symptoms:** Seeds germinated in clumps, uneven growth

**Causes:** Uneven seed distribution

**Solution:** Thin clumps carefully if severe

**Prevention:** Mix seeds with sand for distribution, use shaker

### Problem 4: Damping Off

**Symptoms:** Seedlings collapse

**Causes:** Overwatering, poor drainage

**Solution:** Reduce watering, improve airflow

**Prevention:** Good drainage, don't overwater

## Pro Tips

1. **Temperature is Key** - Arugula is a cool-season crop. Heat makes it bitter and causes bolting. Keep it cool!

2. **Harvest Timing Matters** - Younger = milder, older = spicier. Let customers know they can choose.

3. **Wild vs. Common** - Wild arugula (sylvetta) is slower but more intensely flavored. Try both.

4. **Pizza Connection** - Market to pizzerias. Arugula on pizza is a classic that never goes out of style.

5. **Quick Turnover** - Shorter shelf life means grow to order when possible.

## Scheduling Recommendations

- **Continuous supply:** Plant every 4-5 days
- **Summer challenge** - Difficult in hot weather, may need to pause
- **Winter workhorse** - Thrives in cooler months

## Growing Notes

- **Cool season crop** - Best results spring/fall/winter
- **Can grow near window** - Tolerates lower light
- **Italian favorite** - Strong market in Mediterranean cuisine areas
