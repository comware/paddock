## Overview

Kohlrabi microgreens offer a mild brassica flavor similar to broccoli but with attractive purple-tinged stems (from purple varieties) or clean green appearance (from green varieties). The flavor is sweeter and more delicate than mature kohlrabi bulbs.

Purple kohlrabi is particularly popular because of its striking color - purple stems with green cotyledons create beautiful visual contrast. This variety works well in salad mixes and as a garnish.

Kohlrabi microgreens contain vitamins C and K, fiber, and the beneficial glucosinolates common to all brassicas.

## Best Uses

- Salad mixes
- Garnish (purple variety)
- Sandwich topping
- Asian dishes
- Raw snacking

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Purple Vienna for color
- Green varieties for milder appearance
- Small, round seeds

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- Sealed container
- 3+ years viability

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** No

Small seeds germinate readily without soaking.

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Coco coir, soil, or mats all work well.

**Setup:** 1-2cm moistened medium, level surface.

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 25-35g per 10x20 tray

**Technique:**
1. Even distribution
2. Light misting
3. No covering with medium
4. Light press optional

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 3-4 days

**Method:**
1. Cover with inverted tray
2. Light weight helps
3. Keep at 18-22°C
4. Mist daily

**What to expect:**
- Day 1-2: Germination
- Day 2-3: Stems emerge
- Day 3-4: Purple color begins (purple variety)

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** Stems 2-3cm with cotyledons forming

**Light requirements:**
- Moderate to bright light
- 12-16 hours per day
- Bright light intensifies purple color

**What happens:**
- Color development accelerates
- Cotyledons green up
- Steady growth

### 7. Watering

**Method:** Bottom watering preferred

**Frequency:**
- 1-2x daily
- Keep moist not wet
- Watch for damping off

### 8. Harvest

**Timing indicators:**
- 8-12 days from seeding
- Height: 5-8cm
- Good color development
- Before true leaves

**Technique:**
1. Cut above medium level
2. Morning harvest
3. Handle gently

**Yield:** 200-350g from 10x20 tray

### 9. Post-Harvest

**Handling:**
1. Gentle wash if needed
2. Dry thoroughly
3. Handle carefully

**Storage:**
- Refrigerate at 2-4°C
- Shelf life: 7-10 days
- Color holds well refrigerated

## Common Problems & Solutions

### Problem 1: Poor Color Development (Purple Variety)

**Symptoms:** Stems more green than purple

**Causes:** Insufficient light, too warm

**Solution:** Increase light intensity and duration

**Prevention:** Bright light from start of light phase

### Problem 2: Damping Off

**Symptoms:** Seedlings collapse at base

**Causes:** Overwatering, poor drainage

**Solution:** Improve airflow, reduce watering

**Prevention:** Good drainage, moderate watering

## Pro Tips

1. **Purple is Popular** - Purple Vienna variety commands attention and premium pricing.

2. **Similar to Red Cabbage** - Can be grown interchangeably for color if one variety is unavailable.

3. **Mild Enough for Kids** - Less bitter than some brassicas, good for introducing microgreens.

4. **Reliable Performer** - Not as dramatic as red cabbage but very consistent producer.

---

## Scheduling Recommendations

- **Continuous supply:** Plant every 4-5 days
- **Grows alongside other brassicas** - Same timing as broccoli, kale, red cabbage
- **Year-round availability** - No seasonal limitations with proper lighting
