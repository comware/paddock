## Overview

Red mustard microgreens deliver serious heat - more pungent than yellow mustard and with a complex, lingering spiciness. The leaves often develop beautiful red/purple coloring on the upper surface while remaining green underneath, creating stunning visual appeal.

Varieties like Ruby Streaks, Red Giant, and Osaka Purple each offer slightly different appearances and heat levels, but all share the characteristic mustard punch. The flavor has depth beyond simple heat - there's a horseradish-like quality that develops.

Red mustard is popular with chefs who want bold flavors and striking presentation. It's not for mild palates - this is a statement microgreen.

## Best Uses

- Accent on rich meats (steak, pork)
- Asian cuisine (pairs with soy, ginger)
- Spicy salad component
- Bold garnish
- Craft cocktail garnish (Bloody Marys)

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Red mustard varieties: Ruby Streaks, Red Giant, Osaka Purple
- Small, round, dark brown/reddish seeds
- Variety affects final color intensity

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- Sealed container
- 3+ years viability

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** No

Optional short soak (2-4 hours) can speed germination.

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Any growing medium works. Coco coir or soil preferred.

**Setup:** 1-2cm moistened medium, level surface.

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 25-35g per 10x20 tray

**Technique:**
1. Even distribution
2. Seeds close but not piled
3. Light misting
4. No covering with medium

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 2-3 days

**Method:**
1. Cover with inverted tray
2. Light weight optional
3. Keep at 18-22°C
4. Mist daily

**What to expect:**
- Day 1: Rapid germination begins
- Day 2: Stems emerging, some color visible
- Day 3: Ready for light

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** Stems 2cm with cotyledons forming

**Light requirements:**
- Bright light essential for color development
- 12-16 hours per day
- Light intensity directly affects red coloring

**What happens:**
- Red/purple coloring develops on leaves
- Color intensifies over days
- Strong growth

### 7. Watering

**Method:** Bottom watering preferred

**Frequency:**
- 1-2x daily
- Keep moist
- Tolerates moderate moisture variation

### 8. Harvest

**Timing indicators:**
- 7-10 days from seeding
- Height: 4-6cm
- Maximum color development
- Harvest before true leaves for most tender texture

**Technique:**
1. Cut above medium level
2. Morning harvest
3. Handle to preserve appearance

**Yield:** 250-400g from 10x20 tray

### 9. Post-Harvest

**Handling:**
1. Light washing
2. Dry well
3. Handle carefully to preserve color presentation

**Storage:**
- Refrigerate at 2-4°C
- Shelf life: 7-10 days
- Color holds well when cold

## Common Problems & Solutions

### Problem 1: Poor Red Coloring

**Symptoms:** Leaves mostly green, little red/purple

**Causes:** Insufficient light, too warm

**Solution:** Move closer to lights, cool down

**Prevention:** Bright light from day 1 of light phase

### Problem 2: Too Much Heat (Flavor)

**Symptoms:** Overwhelmingly spicy, unpleasant

**Causes:** Heat stress during growing, harvested too late

**Solution:** Grow cooler, harvest earlier

**Prevention:** Moderate temperatures, timely harvest

### Problem 3: Leggy Growth

**Symptoms:** Tall thin stems, small leaves

**Causes:** Insufficient light, too warm

**Solution:** More light, cooler temps

**Prevention:** Adequate lighting and temperature control

## Pro Tips

1. **Light for Color** - Bright light is essential. Without it, you get green mustard microgreens that just happen to be spicy.

2. **Heat Warning** - Let customers know this is SPICY. Some will love it, others will be surprised.

3. **Small Goes Far** - A little red mustard goes a long way. Market as an accent, not a base green.

4. **Chef Favorite** - Connect with restaurants. Chefs love bold flavors and striking colors.

5. **Bloody Mary Bar** - Bars and restaurants use these for cocktail garnish. Niche but valuable market.
