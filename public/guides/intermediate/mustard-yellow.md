# Mustard (Yellow) Microgreens

## Quick Facts

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Difficulty** | Intermediate |
| **Days to Harvest** | 7-10 days |
| **Blackout Period** | 2-3 days |
| **Pre-soak Required** | No |
| **Seed Density** | 25-35g per 10x20 tray |
| **Expected Yield** | 8-10:1 ratio |
| **Flavor Profile** | Mild mustard heat, tangy, less spicy than red mustard |

## Overview

Yellow mustard microgreens (also called white mustard) provide a gentle introduction to mustard flavors. The heat is milder than red mustard varieties, with a pleasant tangy quality that adds interest without overwhelming other flavors.

The greens have bright green cotyledons with light green to white stems. They grow quickly and reliably, making them a good choice for growers who want mustard flavor without the intense spice.

Yellow mustard seeds are the same ones used to make prepared yellow mustard condiment. The microgreens share some of that characteristic flavor in fresh form.

## Best Uses

- Salad mixes (won't dominate)
- Sandwiches and wraps
- Asian cuisine garnish
- Egg dishes
- Mild spicy accent

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Yellow/white mustard seeds (Sinapis alba)
- Small, round, tan/yellow colored
- Distinct from brown or red mustard

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- Sealed container
- 3+ years viability

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** No

Optional 2-4 hour soak can speed germination.

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Any - very adaptable to different growing media.

**Setup:** 1-2cm moistened medium, level surface.

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 25-35g per 10x20 tray

**Technique:**
1. Even distribution
2. Seeds close but not piled
3. Light misting
4. No covering with medium

**Note:** Mustard seeds are mucilaginous - get slightly sticky when wet.

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 2-3 days (fast germinator)

**Method:**
1. Cover with inverted tray
2. Light weight optional
3. Keep at 18-22°C
4. Mist daily

**What to expect:**
- Day 1: Rapid swelling
- Day 2: Roots and stems emerging
- Day 3: Ready for light, possibly sooner

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** As soon as stems are 2cm with cotyledons forming

**Light requirements:**
- Moderate light sufficient
- 12-16 hours per day
- Not as light-demanding as some varieties

**What happens:**
- Quick greening
- Cotyledons expand
- Fast growth continues

### 7. Watering

**Method:** Bottom watering preferred

**Frequency:**
- 1-2x daily
- Keep moist but not waterlogged
- Mustard tolerates some moisture variation

### 8. Harvest

**Timing indicators:**
- 7-10 days from seeding
- Height: 4-6cm
- Cotyledons fully open
- Before true leaves for mildest flavor

**Technique:**
1. Cut above medium level
2. Morning harvest
3. Quick and clean cut

**Yield:** 250-400g from 10x20 tray

### 9. Post-Harvest

**Handling:**
1. Light washing
2. Dry well
3. Remove any seed hulls

**Storage:**
- Refrigerate at 2-4°C
- Shelf life: 7-10 days
- Maintains quality well

## Common Problems & Solutions

### Problem 1: Slimy Seeds During Germination

**Symptoms:** Seeds stick together, mucilaginous coating

**Causes:** Natural - mustard seeds produce mucilage

**Solution:** Spread quickly before drying, rinse if soaked

**Prevention:** Work quickly, don't over-soak

### Problem 2: Uneven Germination

**Symptoms:** Patchy growth

**Causes:** Uneven distribution, old seeds

**Solution:** Note for next planting

**Prevention:** Even seeding, fresh seeds

### Problem 3: Too Mild Flavor

**Symptoms:** Lacking expected mustard taste

**Causes:** Harvested too early, insufficient light

**Solution:** Let grow slightly longer, more light

**Prevention:** Harvest at full cotyledon development

## Pro Tips

1. **Mild Introduction** - Yellow mustard is a gateway to spicier varieties. Start customers here.

2. **Fast Turnaround** - One of the faster brassicas. Good for filling gaps in production schedule.

3. **Mix with Red** - Combine yellow and red mustard for visual variety and flavor range.

4. **Resilient Grower** - Tolerates some neglect. Good for learning.

5. **Condiment Connection** - Fun marketing angle - "grow your own mustard!"

## Scheduling Recommendations

- **Continuous supply:** Plant every 4-5 days
- **Quick cycle** - Can squeeze in between slower crops
- **Year-round reliable** - No strong seasonal preferences
