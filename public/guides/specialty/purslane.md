## Overview

Purslane is one of the most nutritious greens available - exceptionally high in omega-3 fatty acids (rare for a plant), vitamin E, and other nutrients. As microgreens, purslane produces small succulent leaves with a mild, slightly tangy flavor and satisfying crunch.

The main growing challenge is the extremely tiny seeds - dust-like particles that require careful handling. Once germinated, purslane grows slowly but reliably. The succulent leaves are hardy and have excellent shelf life.

Purslane is increasingly popular in health food markets where its omega-3 content is a significant selling point.

## Best Uses

- Health food market (omega-3 content)
- Succulent texture in salads
- Mediterranean cuisine (traditional ingredient)
- Nutritional supplement
- Mild-flavored crunchy green

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Purslane seeds (Portulaca oleracea)
- Extremely tiny, black, shiny seeds
- Golden purslane variety has yellow-green leaves

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- 3-5 years viability (good keepers)

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** No - seeds too tiny to handle when wet

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Very fine seed-starting mix, perfectly smooth

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 5-10g per 10x20 tray (very tiny seeds!)

**Technique:**
1. Mix seeds with fine sand for distribution
2. Use shaker or sieve
3. Do NOT cover - needs light
4. Mist extremely gently

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 4-5 days (minimal - benefits from light)

Loose cover only. Some light penetration OK.

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** Early - purslane likes light

Moderate to bright light, 12-16 hours. Warmth helps.

### 7. Watering

Gentle misting. Don't overwater - succulent stores water.

### 8. Harvest

**Timing:** 14-21 days, height 3-5cm, succulent leaves visible

**Yield:** 50-100g from 10x20 tray (low but nutritious)

### 9. Post-Harvest

Succulent leaves are hardy. Refrigerate, 10-14 days shelf life (excellent!).

## Pro Tips

1. **Omega-3 Star** - Highest plant source of omega-3s. Major selling point.

2. **Warmth Loving** - Unlike most microgreens, purslane thrives in heat.

3. **Long Shelf Life** - Succulent leaves last longer than delicate microgreens.

4. **Tiny Seeds** - Mix with sand. Handle like dust.

5. **Mediterranean Heritage** - Traditional ingredient in Mediterranean diets.
