## Overview

Shungiku (edible chrysanthemum, crown daisy, chop suey greens) is a traditional Asian vegetable with a unique flavor that's herbaceous, slightly bitter, and unmistakably chrysanthemum. As microgreens, the distinctive flavor comes through clearly.

The flavor is polarizing - some love it immediately, others need time to appreciate it. It's essential in certain Asian dishes, particularly Japanese hot pot (nabemono) and Korean cooking.

Growing shungiku is moderately straightforward, though the plants prefer cooler conditions and may bolt in heat.

## Best Uses

- Japanese cuisine (hot pot)
- Korean cuisine
- Chinese cooking
- Unique Asian flavor profile
- Specialty restaurant market

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Shungiku/Chrysanthemum seeds (edible variety)
- Not ornamental chrysanthemums
- Fresh seeds preferred

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- Use within 1-2 years

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** No

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Fine coco coir or soil

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 20-30g per 10x20 tray

Spread evenly, press lightly, do not cover.

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 4-5 days

Keep COOL (15-20°C). Doesn't like heat.

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** When germination visible

Moderate light, 12-14 hours. Keep cool.

### 7. Watering

Keep moist. Cool conditions.

### 8. Harvest

**Timing:** 14-21 days, height 5-8cm, distinctive leaves visible

**Yield:** 100-200g from 10x20 tray

### 9. Post-Harvest

Refrigerate immediately. 5-7 days shelf life.

## Pro Tips

1. **Cool Weather Crop** - Bolts in heat. Spring/fall or climate control.

2. **Unique Flavor** - Nothing else tastes like shungiku.

3. **Asian Market Essential** - Some dishes require it specifically.

4. **Sample First** - Polarizing flavor. Sample before large orders.

5. **Edible Variety** - Ensure you have culinary chrysanthemum, not ornamental.
