| Attribute | Value |

| **Pre-soak Required** | Yes (12-24 hours) |

| **Flavor Profile** | Mild corn sweetness, larger shoots than popcorn |

## Overview

Field corn (dent corn, flint corn) produces larger, more substantial shoots than popcorn while maintaining mild corn flavor. Decorative varieties like Indian corn or blue corn can produce colorful shoots with purple or red tints.

Growing is identical to popcorn - straightforward with high yields. The main advantage of field corn varieties is the potential for color (from pigmented varieties) and larger shoot size.

Field corn is even more economical than popcorn as seeds are available in bulk from feed stores.

## Best Uses

- Colored microgreens (from pigmented varieties)
- Substantial corn shoots
- Very economical production
- Bulk applications
- Southwestern cuisine

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Field corn, dent corn, or decorative corn
- Blue corn or Indian corn for color
- Untreated seed (important!)
- Feed corn works but ensure no chemical treatment

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- Years of viability

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** Yes - larger kernels need longer

**Process:**
1. Rinse thoroughly
2. Soak 12-24 hours
3. Kernels should feel soft
4. Drain completely

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Coco coir, soil, or hemp mat

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 200-350g per 10x20 tray

Single dense layer, press in firmly.

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 4-5 days

Cover, keep warm (20-25°C), moist.

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** When shoots 5-8cm tall

Light brings out color in pigmented varieties.

### 7. Watering

Keep moist. Large seeds hold moisture - don't overwater.

### 8. Harvest

**Timing:** 10-14 days, height 15-20cm, before toughening

**Yield:** 1.5-3kg from 10x20 tray (excellent)

### 9. Post-Harvest

Cut at soil level. Refrigerate, 3-5 days shelf life.

## Pro Tips

1. **Blue Corn Color** - Blue and red corn varieties can produce tinted shoots.

2. **Untreated Seed** - Ensure seeds aren't treated with fungicides.

3. **Very Economical** - Feed store prices for bulk corn are minimal.

4. **Larger Than Popcorn** - More substantial shoots for some applications.

5. **Light for Color** - Pigmented varieties need light to develop color.
