# Flax Microgreens

## Quick Facts

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Difficulty** | Advanced |
| **Days to Harvest** | 10-14 days |
| **Blackout Period** | 3-4 days |
| **Pre-soak Required** | No (mucilaginous!) |
| **Seed Density** | 40-60g per 10x20 tray |
| **Expected Yield** | 4-6:1 ratio |
| **Flavor Profile** | Mild, nutty, with nutritional benefits |

## Overview

Flax microgreens are nutritious but challenging due to the seeds' mucilaginous nature - when wet, they produce a thick, gel-like coating that can suffocate germination if not managed properly. This same mucilage is what makes flax seeds nutritious (soluble fiber), but it creates growing challenges.

Despite the difficulty, flax microgreens are worth growing for health-conscious markets. They're high in omega-3s, fiber, and lignans. The mild, nutty flavor is pleasant and versatile.

Success with flax requires specific techniques to manage the mucilage layer.

## Best Uses

- Health food market
- Omega-3 content marketing
- Mild nutty flavor applications
- Smoothie addition
- Nutritional supplement

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Flax seeds (brown or golden)
- Small, flat, shiny seeds
- Food-grade is fine

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- 2-3 years viability

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** NO - Do NOT pre-soak!

Pre-soaking creates thick mucilage gel that prevents germination. Plant dry.

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Coco coir or soil with EXCELLENT drainage

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 40-60g per 10x20 tray

**Critical technique:**
1. Spread dry seeds evenly
2. Mist LIGHTLY - just enough to trigger germination
3. The gel will form - this is normal
4. Do NOT over-mist

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 3-4 days

**Managing mucilage:**
1. Keep humidity moderate (not high)
2. Good airflow essential
3. Don't add more water until gel absorbs
4. Some seeds won't germinate - normal

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** When shoots emerge through gel

Moderate light, 12-14 hours.

### 7. Watering

**Critical:** Water from bottom only. Top watering disturbs gel and seedlings.

### 8. Harvest

**Timing:** 10-14 days, height 5-8cm, thin grass-like shoots

**Yield:** 150-300g from 10x20 tray

### 9. Post-Harvest

Rinse gently if any gel residue. Refrigerate, 5-7 days.

## Common Problems & Solutions

### Problem 1: Seeds Suffocate in Gel

**Symptoms:** Seeds don't germinate, moldy gel mass

**Causes:** Too much water, poor drainage

**Solution:** Start over with less water, better drainage

**Prevention:** Mist very lightly, never soak

### Problem 2: Mold in Mucilage

**Symptoms:** Mold growing in gel

**Causes:** Too wet, poor airflow

**Solution:** Improve ventilation, reduce moisture

**Prevention:** Good airflow, moderate humidity

---

## Pro Tips

1. **Never Soak** - Unlike most seeds, flax must NOT be pre-soaked.

2. **Less Water** - Use less water than you think. Gel holds moisture.

3. **Bottom Water Only** - Once established, only water from below.

4. **Accept Some Loss** - Not all seeds will germinate through mucilage.

5. **Health Marketing** - Omega-3s and lignans are major selling points.
