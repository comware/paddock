## Overview

Quinoa microgreens leverage the powerful "superfood" marketing status of quinoa while being relatively easy to grow. The small seeds produce delicate shoots with mild flavor and the name recognition that drives health-conscious consumers.

Growing quinoa is straightforward - the main consideration is rinsing seeds well to remove saponins (bitter coating) before planting. The plants grow quickly and reliably.

The combination of easy growing and strong marketing appeal makes quinoa an attractive crop for health food markets.

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Quinoa seeds (white, red, or black)
- Unwashed preferred (can rinse yourself)
- Food-grade quinoa works

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- 2-3 years viability

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** Yes - also removes saponins

**Process:**
1. Rinse thoroughly multiple times
2. Water should run clear (removing saponins)
3. Soak 4-8 hours
4. Rinse again, drain completely

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Fine coco coir or seed-starting mix

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 25-40g per 10x20 tray

Spread evenly, press lightly. Small seeds.

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 3-4 days

Standard conditions, 18-22°C, moist.

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** When germination complete

Moderate light, 12-14 hours. Red quinoa may show color.

### 7. Watering

Keep moist. Gentle watering for small seeds.

### 8. Harvest

**Timing:** 10-14 days, height 5-8cm, leaves developed

**Yield:** 150-300g from 10x20 tray

### 9. Post-Harvest

Handle gently - delicate. Refrigerate, 5-7 days.
