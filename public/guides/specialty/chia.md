# Chia Microgreens

## Quick Facts

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Difficulty** | Advanced |
| **Days to Harvest** | 10-14 days |
| **Blackout Period** | 3-4 days |
| **Pre-soak Required** | No (mucilaginous!) |
| **Seed Density** | 25-40g per 10x20 tray |
| **Expected Yield** | 5-7:1 ratio |
| **Flavor Profile** | Mild, slightly grassy, with superfood status |

## Overview

Chia microgreens share the same mucilaginous challenge as flax - the seeds form a thick gel coating when wet that can prevent germination. This is the same property that makes chia pudding popular, but it makes microgreen growing tricky.

Chia's "superfood" marketing status makes it attractive to health-conscious customers. The microgreens are mild-flavored with good nutrition, and the name recognition helps with sales.

Growing chia requires the same careful moisture management as flax.

## Best Uses

- Superfood marketing
- Health food market
- Smoothie addition
- Mild microgreen mix component
- Marketing-driven sales

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Chia seeds (Salvia hispanica)
- Tiny, oval, speckled gray/black seeds
- Food-grade is fine

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- 2-4 years viability

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** NO - Do NOT pre-soak!

Pre-soaking creates impossible-to-handle gel mass.

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Well-draining coco coir or terracotta saucer

**Note:** Some growers use unglazed terracotta trays which absorb excess moisture.

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 25-40g per 10x20 tray

**Critical technique:**
1. Spread dry seeds thinly and evenly
2. Mist VERY lightly
3. Gel will form immediately
4. Don't add more water until needed

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 3-4 days

Good airflow, moderate humidity, patience.

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** When shoots emerge

Moderate light, 12-14 hours.

### 7. Watering

Bottom watering only once established. Minimal water.

### 8. Harvest

**Timing:** 10-14 days, height 4-6cm, small leaves developed

**Yield:** 100-250g from 10x20 tray

### 9. Post-Harvest

Rinse if needed. Refrigerate, 5-7 days.

## Pro Tips

1. **Never Soak** - Same rule as flax. No pre-soaking.

2. **Superfood Status** - Name recognition helps sales.

3. **Terracotta Trick** - Unglazed terracotta absorbs excess moisture.

4. **Thin Seeding** - Less is more with mucilaginous seeds.

5. **Patience** - Germination through gel is slow.
