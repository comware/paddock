## Overview

Oat grass (oat microgreens) is essentially the oat equivalent of wheatgrass - young grass shoots with mild, sweet flavor. Compared to wheatgrass, oat grass is milder and slightly sweeter, making it more palatable for those who find wheatgrass too intense.

Growing oat grass is straightforward and economical. Oat groats (hulled oats) or unhulled oat seeds both work, though unhulled seeds may have slightly better germination.

Oat grass is popular in juice bars and for those seeking a milder alternative to wheatgrass.

## Best Uses

- Wheatgrass alternative (milder)
- Juice bar ingredient
- Smoothie addition
- Health food market
- Pet grass (cats and dogs)

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Oat groats (hulled) or whole oat seeds (unhulled)
- Not quick oats or rolled oats (won't sprout)
- Feed oats work if untreated

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- 2-3 years viability

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** Yes

**Process:**
1. Rinse thoroughly
2. Soak 8-12 hours
3. Seeds will swell
4. Drain completely

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Coco coir, soil, or hemp mat

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 150-200g per 10x20 tray

Spread evenly, press into medium.

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 3-4 days

Standard conditions, keep moist.

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** When shoots 5-7cm tall

Moderate light for green color development.

### 7. Watering

Keep moist. Grass likes consistent moisture.

### 8. Harvest

**Timing:** 10-14 days, height 12-18cm, bright green blades

**Yield:** 800g-1.5kg from 10x20 tray

### 9. Post-Harvest

Cut at soil level. Use quickly or juice immediately. 3-5 days refrigerated.

## Pro Tips

1. **Milder Than Wheat** - Good alternative for wheatgrass-sensitive customers.

2. **Not Rolled Oats** - Must use whole oats, not processed.

3. **Pet Grass** - Popular for cats and dogs - secondary market.

4. **Juice Quickly** - Best used fresh or juiced immediately.

5. **Affordable** - Oat seeds are very economical.
