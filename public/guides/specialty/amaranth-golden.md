# Golden Amaranth Microgreens

## Quick Facts

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Difficulty** | Intermediate |
| **Days to Harvest** | 10-14 days |
| **Blackout Period** | 3-4 days |
| **Pre-soak Required** | No |
| **Seed Density** | 10-15g per 10x20 tray |
| **Expected Yield** | 4-6:1 ratio |
| **Flavor Profile** | Mild, earthy, spinach-like with golden/yellow color |

## Overview

Golden amaranth is a color variant that produces yellow to golden shoots instead of the more common red or green. The flavor is identical to other amaranths - mild, earthy, spinach-like - but the golden color provides visual differentiation.

Growing requirements are the same as red amaranth - tiny seeds that need careful handling, light for germination, and warm conditions. The golden color develops best with good light exposure.

Golden amaranth can be mixed with red and green varieties for a tricolor effect.

## Best Uses

- Color variety in amaranth offerings
- Tricolor mix component
- Visual differentiation
- Same applications as other amaranths
- Photography and plating

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Golden or yellow amaranth variety
- Tiny seeds (same as red/green)
- Fresh seeds for best germination

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- Use within 1 year

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** No - seeds too tiny

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Very fine coco coir or seed-starting mix

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 10-15g per 10x20 tray

Mix with sand for distribution. Do NOT cover.

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 3-4 days (minimal)

Loose cover, some light OK. Keep warm (20-25°C).

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** Early - when germination begins

Bright light for best color development.

### 7. Watering

Very gentle misting or bottom watering.

### 8. Harvest

**Timing:** 10-14 days, height 4-6cm, golden color developed

**Yield:** 50-100g from 10x20 tray

### 9. Post-Harvest

Handle gently. Refrigerate, 5-7 days.

## Pro Tips

1. **Same as Red** - Growing identical to red amaranth.

2. **Tricolor Potential** - Combine with red and green for visual impact.

3. **Light for Color** - Bright light brings out golden hue.

4. **Tiny Seeds** - Handle like red amaranth - mix with sand.

5. **Visual Focus** - Color is the differentiation, not flavor.
