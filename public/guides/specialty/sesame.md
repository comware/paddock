# Sesame Microgreens

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Difficulty** | Advanced |
| **Days to Harvest** | 12-18 days |
| **Blackout Period** | 4-5 days |
| **Pre-soak Required** | Yes (4-8 hours) |
| **Seed Density** | 25-40g per 10x20 tray |
| **Expected Yield** | 4-6:1 ratio |
| **Flavor Profile** | Mild nutty sesame flavor, delicate |

## Overview

Sesame microgreens are a specialty crop that captures the subtle nutty flavor of sesame in microgreen form. The tiny seeds produce delicate shoots that hint at the familiar sesame taste.

Growing sesame is challenging - the seeds are small, germination can be uneven, and the plants are delicate. Sesame also prefers warm conditions and doesn't tolerate cool temperatures well.

For growers serving Asian cuisine markets, sesame microgreens offer authentic flavor that complements sesame-heavy dishes.

## Best Uses

- Asian cuisine
- Sesame-flavored dishes
- Sushi accompaniment
- Middle Eastern cuisine
- Specialty flavor applications

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Raw, unhulled sesame seeds
- Not toasted (won't germinate)
- Black or white sesame both work

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- Use within 1-2 years

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** Yes

Soak 4-8 hours, drain thoroughly.

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Fine seed-starting mix

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 25-40g per 10x20 tray

Spread evenly, do not cover.

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 4-5 days

Keep WARM (22-28°C). Sesame likes heat.

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** When germination visible

Moderate light, warm conditions.

### 7. Watering

Keep moist but not wet. Delicate seedlings.

### 8. Harvest

**Timing:** 12-18 days, height 4-6cm, leaves developed

**Yield:** 100-200g from 10x20 tray (low)

### 9. Post-Harvest

Very delicate. Refrigerate, 5-7 days.

## Pro Tips

1. **Warmth Essential** - Sesame is tropical. Keep warm throughout.

2. **Raw Seeds Only** - Toasted sesame won't germinate.

3. **Black Sesame** - Black variety may show color in stems.

4. **Asian Market** - Natural fit with sesame-heavy Asian cuisines.

5. **Challenging Crop** - Not for beginners. Master basics first.
