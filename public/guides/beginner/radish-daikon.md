# Radish (Daikon) Microgreens

## Quick Facts

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Difficulty** | Beginner |
| **Days to Harvest** | 6-10 days |
| **Blackout Period** | 2-3 days |
| **Pre-soak Required** | No |
| **Seed Density** | 30-40g per 10x20 tray |
| **Expected Yield** | 8-10:1 ratio |
| **Flavor Profile** | Mild radish flavor, less spicy than China Rose |

## Overview

Daikon radish microgreens offer all the ease of growing that radish is known for, with a milder, more approachable flavor than the spicy China Rose variety. The white stems and bright green cotyledons create a clean, attractive appearance.

This variety is excellent for those who want radish's nutritional benefits and fast growth cycle but prefer a gentler flavor that won't overpower dishes. Daikon microgreens work well for customers who find other radish varieties too peppery.

Like all radish microgreens, Daikon is packed with vitamins and minerals, plus beneficial glucosinolates. The milder flavor makes it more versatile in the kitchen.

## Best Uses

- Mixed salads (won't overpower)
- Sushi and Japanese cuisine
- Light soups as garnish
- Spring rolls
- Mild flavor base in microgreen mixes

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Daikon radish seeds (white radish variety)
- Larger than China Rose seeds
- Clean, uniform appearance

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- Sealed container
- Long viability (3+ years)

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** No

Optional 2-4 hour soak can speed germination slightly.

### 3. Tray Preparation

Same as China Rose - any medium works well.

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 30-40g per 10x20 tray

Distribute evenly, press lightly, mist surface.

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 2-3 days

Same protocol as China Rose. Quick germinators.

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** After 2-3 days when cotyledons forming

Good light develops best appearance - white stems, green leaves.

### 7. Watering

Bottom water 1-2x daily. Don't overwater - damping off risk.

### 8. Harvest

**Timing:** 6-10 days, height 4-6cm, before true leaves

**Yield:** 250-400g from 10x20 tray

### 9. Post-Harvest

Excellent shelf life (7-10 days refrigerated).

## Common Problems & Solutions

Same as China Rose radish - damping off is primary concern. Avoid overwatering.
