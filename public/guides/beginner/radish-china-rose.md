# Radish (China Rose) Microgreens

## Quick Facts

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Difficulty** | Beginner |
| **Days to Harvest** | 6-10 days |
| **Blackout Period** | 2-3 days |
| **Pre-soak Required** | No |
| **Seed Density** | 30-40g per 10x20 tray |
| **Expected Yield** | 8-10:1 ratio |
| **Flavor Profile** | Spicy, peppery kick with pink stems |

## Overview

China Rose radish microgreens are one of the fastest and easiest varieties to grow. They germinate rapidly, grow vigorously, and are ready to harvest in under two weeks. The striking pink stems and green cotyledons make them visually appealing, while their spicy flavor adds punch to any dish.

This variety is particularly forgiving for beginners due to its robust nature and fast growth cycle. The quick turnaround also means you can experiment frequently and learn rapidly.

Radish microgreens are rich in vitamins A, B, C, E, and K, plus minerals like calcium, iron, and phosphorus. The characteristic spiciness comes from glucosinolates, which have documented health benefits.

## Best Uses

- Salads for color and spice
- Tacos and Mexican cuisine
- Asian dishes and sushi garnish
- Sandwich topping for kick
- Burger garnish

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- China Rose radish seeds (specific variety)
- Small, round, reddish-brown seeds
- Should be uniform in size

**Storage:**
- Room temperature in dry location
- Sealed container
- 3+ years viability

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** No - radish seeds germinate quickly without soaking.

Optional: A 2-4 hour soak can speed germination by 12-24 hours if desired.

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium options:**
- Any growing medium works well
- Coco coir, hemp mats, or soil
- Mats provide cleanest harvest

**Setup:**
1. Prepare tray with 1-2cm of medium
2. Moisten thoroughly
3. Level surface

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 30-40g per 10x20 tray (lighter than sunflower/pea)

**Technique:**
1. Sprinkle seeds evenly across surface
2. Seeds should be close but not overlapping
3. Press lightly into medium
4. Mist surface

**Note:** Radish seeds are small - even distribution is key.

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 2-3 days (shortest of beginner varieties)

**Method:**
1. Cover tray - weight optional for radish
2. Keep in dark at 18-24°C
3. Mist daily

**What to expect:**
- Day 1: Seeds swell, radicles emerge
- Day 2: White stems visible, cotyledons forming
- Day 3: Ready to uncover (2-3cm tall)

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** When cotyledons are forming (2-3 days)

**Light requirements:**
- Moderate to bright light
- 12-16 hours per day
- Develops best color with strong light

**What happens:**
- Pink coloring develops in stems
- Cotyledons open and green up
- Very fast growth (visible daily change)

### 7. Watering

**Method:** Bottom watering or careful top misting

**Frequency:**
- Once or twice daily in light phase
- Don't overwater - radish is prone to damping off
- Allow slight drying between waterings

### 8. Harvest

**Timing indicators:**
- 6-10 days from seeding
- Height: 4-6cm
- Cotyledons fully open
- Pink stem color developed
- Before true leaves emerge (maintains tenderness)

**Technique:**
1. Cut just above medium with sharp scissors
2. Harvest early morning for best crunch
3. Can harvest all at once - very fast crop

**Yield:** 250-400g from 10x20 tray

### 9. Post-Harvest

**Handling:**
1. Shake gently to remove seed hulls
2. Quick rinse if needed
3. Dry thoroughly

**Storage:**
- Refrigerate immediately
- Shelf life: 7-10 days (one of the longer-lasting varieties)
- Maintains crunch well

## Common Problems & Solutions

### Problem 1: Damping Off

**Symptoms:** Seedlings fall over, stems thin and brown at base

**Causes:** Overwatering, poor drainage, too warm and wet

**Solution:** Remove affected plants, improve airflow, reduce watering

**Prevention:** Don't overwater, ensure good drainage, moderate temperature

### Problem 2: Pale/Weak Color

**Symptoms:** Stems not developing pink color

**Causes:** Insufficient light

**Solution:** Move closer to light, increase light duration

**Prevention:** Ensure adequate light from day 1 of light phase

### Problem 3: Bitter Taste

**Symptoms:** Overly spicy or bitter flavor

**Causes:** Stress from heat or irregular watering, grown too long

**Solution:** Harvest earlier, maintain consistent conditions

**Prevention:** Keep cool, water consistently, harvest at peak timing

## Pro Tips

1. **Quick Crop for Testing** - Use radish to test new growing setups. Fast turnaround means quick feedback.

2. **Color = Light** - The pink coloring develops in response to light. More light = more vibrant pink.

3. **Spice Level Control** - Harvest earlier for milder flavor, later for more spice.

4. **Great Mixer** - The pink color contrasts beautifully with green microgreens in salad mixes.

5. **Heat-Tolerant** - One of the more heat-tolerant microgreens, good for summer growing.

## Scheduling Recommendations

- **Fastest turnaround:** Plant every 4-5 days for continuous supply
- **Good teaching crop:** Quick results maintain motivation
- **Summer workhorse:** Tolerates heat better than most

## Companion Planting

Grows well with:

- **Sunflower** - Radish ready first, can be mixed in final week
