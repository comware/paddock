# Wheatgrass Microgreens

## Quick Facts

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Difficulty** | Beginner |
| **Days to Harvest** | 7-10 days |
| **Blackout Period** | 2-3 days |
| **Pre-soak Required** | Yes (8-12 hours) |
| **Seed Density** | 150-200g per 10x20 tray |
| **Expected Yield** | 5-7:1 ratio |
| **Flavor Profile** | Sweet, grassy, mild "green" taste |

## Overview

Wheatgrass is one of the most popular microgreens, primarily grown for juicing. Unlike other microgreens that are eaten whole, wheatgrass is typically juiced because the fibrous blades are difficult to digest when eaten raw. The bright green juice has a sweet, grassy flavor.

This is one of the easiest crops to grow successfully. The seeds are inexpensive, germinate reliably, and grow quickly. Wheatgrass is very forgiving of temperature variations and minor inconsistencies in care.

Wheatgrass juice is renowned for its nutritional density - packed with chlorophyll, vitamins A, C, and E, iron, calcium, and amino acids. It's a staple of juice bars and health food establishments worldwide.

**Note:** Contains gluten - not suitable for those with celiac disease or gluten sensitivity.

## Best Uses

- Fresh juice (primary use)
- Juice shots
- Smoothie addition
- Pet grass (cats and dogs love it)
- Decorative living display

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Hard red winter wheat or soft wheat
- Whole wheat berries, not flour or processed
- Organic preferred for juicing applications

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- Very long viability (5+ years properly stored)
- Buy in bulk - very affordable

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** Yes - essential for wheatgrass

**Process:**
1. Measure 150-200g of wheat berries
2. Rinse thoroughly
3. Soak in 3x volume of water for 8-12 hours
4. Seeds will swell significantly
5. Drain and rinse before planting
6. Optional: Let sit damp for 12-24 hours after draining to start sprouting

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium options:**
- Soil gives highest yield
- Coco coir works well
- Can be grown hydroponically on mats

**Setup:**
1. Use deeper medium (3-4cm) for stronger roots
2. Moisten thoroughly
3. Level surface

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 150-200g per 10x20 tray (dense carpet)

**Technique:**
1. Spread soaked seeds very densely
2. Can be 2-3 seeds deep - wheatgrass handles crowding
3. Press into medium
4. Mist heavily

**Note:** Denser seeding = easier juicing but watch for mold.

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 2-3 days

**Method:**
1. Cover with tray or dome
2. No weight needed
3. Keep at 18-24°C
4. Mist 2x daily - wheat drinks a lot

**What to expect:**
- Day 1: Seeds swell more, white roots emerge
- Day 2: Pale shoots appear
- Day 3: Dense grass 2-3cm tall

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** When grass is 2-3cm tall (day 2-3)

**Light requirements:**
- Bright light produces deepest green color
- 12-16 hours per day
- Can tolerate lower light than most greens

**What happens:**
- Rapid greening within hours
- Growth accelerates significantly
- Dense grass carpet develops

### 7. Watering

**Method:** Heavy bottom watering

**Frequency:**
- 2-3x daily in light phase
- Wheatgrass is very thirsty
- Never let dry out - grass yellows quickly

**Tip:** Can water from top - grass is sturdy and won't damage.

### 8. Harvest

**Timing indicators:**
- 7-10 days from seeding
- Height: 15-20cm (6-8 inches)
- Just before "jointing" (second blade emerging)
- This is peak nutrition and sweetness

**Technique:**
1. Cut about 1cm above soil with scissors
2. Cut just before juicing for freshest juice
3. Can do second cutting (lower quality, still usable)

**Yield:** 400-600g of grass from 10x20 tray

**Juice yield:** Approximately 60-90ml per tray

### 9. Post-Harvest

**Handling:**
1. Juice immediately for best nutrition
2. Can store cut grass refrigerated 5-7 days
3. Fresh juice oxidizes quickly - drink within 15 minutes

**Storage of juice:**
- Fresh is best (drink immediately)
- Frozen cubes last 2-3 months
- Refrigerated juice: 24 hours maximum

## Common Problems & Solutions

### Problem 1: Mold at Soil Level

**Symptoms:** White fuzzy mold between grass blades at base

**Causes:** Too wet, poor airflow, very dense seeding

**Solution:** Increase airflow, reduce watering, harvest early if mild

**Prevention:** Good ventilation, don't overwater, consider slightly lower density

### Problem 2: Yellow Tips

**Symptoms:** Grass tips turn yellow

**Causes:** Underwatering, too hot, or past peak harvest time

**Solution:** Water more frequently, cool down, harvest immediately

**Prevention:** Consistent heavy watering, moderate temperature

### Problem 3: Slow/Uneven Growth

**Symptoms:** Patchy germination, slow growth

**Causes:** Old seeds, insufficient soaking, too cold

**Solution:** Use fresh seeds, soak longer, increase temperature

**Prevention:** Quality seeds, full 8-12 hour soak, 18-24°C

### Problem 4: Bitter Taste

**Symptoms:** Juice tastes bitter rather than sweet

**Causes:** Grown past peak, heat stress, underwatered

**Solution:** Harvest earlier next time, address environmental factors

**Prevention:** Harvest at 15-20cm before jointing, consistent care

## Pro Tips

1. **Juice Fresh** - Wheatgrass juice oxidizes rapidly. Cut and juice within minutes for best taste and nutrition.

2. **Second Cut** - You can get a second (smaller) harvest. Quality is lower but still usable for juice.

3. **Pet Grass Bonus** - Unsold/excess wheatgrass can be sold as pet grass for cats and dogs.

4. **Masticating Juicer** - Get much better juice yield with a masticating juicer vs. centrifugal.

5. **Freeze for Later** - Make juice ice cubes for storage. Thaw and drink immediately.

6. **Dense = Easier Juice** - Very dense trays are harder to maintain (mold risk) but easier to cut and juice.

## Scheduling Recommendations

- **Juice bar supply:** Plant 2-3 trays every 3-4 days
- **Personal use:** One tray per week is plenty
- **Peak at day 8-9** - Plan around this timing

## Growing Notes

- **Cheapest microgreen to grow** - Very affordable seeds
- **High water usage** - Factor into growing space planning
- **Can grow on countertop** - Popular home growing project
- **Reuse soil once** - Can replant in same soil one more time
