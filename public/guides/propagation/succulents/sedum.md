# Sedum (Stonecrop) Propagation

## Quick Facts

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Difficulty** | Very Easy |
| **Best Method** | Stem cuttings |
| **Alternative Methods** | Division, leaf cuttings |
| **Time to Root** | 1-3 weeks |
| **Best Season** | Spring to autumn |
| **Success Rate** | 95-100% typical |
| **Rooting Hormone** | Not needed |

## Overview

Sedums are perhaps the easiest of all succulents to propagate. Stem cuttings root so readily that dropped pieces often take root on their own. The phrase "set it and forget it" applies well to sedum propagation.

The wide variety of sedums - from groundcovers like S. acre to tall sedums like 'Autumn Joy' - all propagate easily from cuttings. Some root from leaves; all root from stems; and clumping types divide readily.

This forgiving nature makes sedums ideal for beginners and for creating large quantities of plants for groundcover or green roof applications.

## Best Uses for Propagated Plants

- Groundcover mass plantings
- Green roof projects
- Container combinations
- Rock gardens
- Sharing with friends

## Propagation Methods

### Stem Cuttings (Recommended)

**Overview:** Fast and nearly foolproof method.

**When to take material:**
- Best months: September to April (spring to autumn)
- Works almost any time except deep winter

**Step-by-step process:**

1. **Select material**
   - Healthy stems 5-10cm long
   - Any part of the plant
   - Avoid flowering stems if possible

2. **Take cuttings**
   - Cut or snap stems
   - Multiple cuttings from one stem fine
   - No precision needed

3. **Optional callusing**
   - Sedums often don't need callusing
   - 1-2 days if desired
   - Can plant fresh-cut in dry conditions

4. **Plant cuttings**
   - Insert in well-drained mix
   - Or lay on soil surface
   - Even just scattered on ground works

5. **Minimal care**
   - Light watering
   - Sun or part shade
   - Rooting in 1-3 weeks
   - Some species root in days

6. **That's it**
   - Sedums are remarkably easy
   - Little can go wrong
   - New growth indicates success

### Division (For Clumping Types)

**When to use:** For established clumping sedums.

**Process:**
1. Dig up clump in spring or autumn
2. Pull or cut into sections
3. Replant immediately
4. Water in
5. Virtually 100% success

### Leaf Cuttings (Some Species)

**When to use:** For thick-leaved types when stems unavailable.

**Process:**
1. Remove plump leaf
2. Let callus 1-2 days
3. Lay on soil surface
4. Mist occasionally
5. Slower than stem cuttings
6. Works for some but not all species

## Common Problems & Solutions

### Problem 1: Cuttings Rot

**Symptoms:** Stems turn mushy and black.

**Causes:** Too much water, poor drainage.

**Solution:** Reduce water, improve drainage.

**Prevention:** Sedums need very little water to root.

### Problem 2: Sprawling, Leggy Growth

**Symptoms:** Plants stretch and flop.

**Causes:** Insufficient light.

**Solution:** Move to sunnier position.

**Prevention:** Full sun produces compact, sturdy growth.

### Problem 3: This Is Not a Problem Section

**Symptoms:** Everything works fine.

**Causes:** Sedums are just that easy.

**Solution:** Enjoy your new plants.

**Prevention:** None needed.

## Pro Tips

1. **The easiest succulent** - If you're new to succulents, start with sedum. Success builds confidence.

2. **Scatter and forget** - For groundcover, scatter stem pieces on prepared soil. Many will root where they land.

3. **Flowering stems work** - Though vegetative stems are ideal, flowering stems will often root too, just remove spent flowers.

4. **Green roof applications** - Sedums are the backbone of extensive green roofs due to their easy propagation and drought tolerance.

5. **Winter interest** - Even deciduous sedums propagate easily. 'Autumn Joy' clumps divide readily in spring.

## Mother Plant Care

### Maintaining Stock Plants

Sedums are low-maintenance:

- **Position:** Full sun for best form.
- **Watering:** Drought-tolerant once established.
- **Feeding:** Minimal - lean soil is fine.
- **Pruning:** Provides more cutting material.

### Selecting Material

Choose material that displays:

- Healthy growth
- Good color
- Firm leaves
- Any healthy portion works
