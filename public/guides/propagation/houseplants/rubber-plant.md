# Rubber Plant Propagation

## Quick Facts

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Difficulty** | Easy to Intermediate |
| **Best Method** | Stem cuttings |
| **Alternative Methods** | Air layering |
| **Time to Root** | 4-8 weeks |
| **Best Season** | Spring to summer |
| **Success Rate** | 70-85% typical |
| **Rooting Hormone** | Recommended |

## Overview

Rubber plants (Ficus elastica) propagate more readily than their fiddle leaf fig relatives. Stem cuttings root with reasonable success, and air layering offers near-guaranteed results for valuable specimens.

Like all Ficus, rubber plants exude milky latex sap when cut. This sap should be rinsed or allowed to dry before planting to prevent rot and reduce mess. The sap can irritate skin - handle with care.

The various coloured cultivars ('Burgundy', 'Tineke', 'Ruby') propagate using identical methods, though variegated types may be slightly slower.

## Best Uses for Propagated Plants

- Managing overgrown plants
- Creating fuller specimens
- Sharing with friends
- Maintaining favourite cultivars

## Propagation Methods

### Stem Cuttings (Recommended)

**Overview:** Straightforward method with good success.

**When to take material:**
- Best months: September to January (spring to midsummer)
- During active growth

**Step-by-step process:**

1. **Select cutting material**
   - 15-20cm stem sections
   - Include 2-3 leaves
   - Healthy, mature growth

2. **Take cuttings**
   - Cut with clean, sharp blade
   - Expect milky sap - normal
   - Rinse cut end or let dry 30-60 minutes

3. **Prepare for rooting**
   - Remove lower leaves
   - Roll remaining leaves and secure with rubber band (reduces water loss)
   - Apply rooting hormone to cut end

4. **Plant cutting**
   - Well-draining propagation mix
   - Insert 5-8cm deep
   - Water lightly

5. **Create humidity**
   - Cover with plastic bag or propagator
   - Bright indirect light
   - Warm conditions (22-28°C)

6. **Ongoing care**
   - Keep soil barely moist
   - Ventilate occasionally
   - Roots develop in 4-8 weeks

7. **Confirm and pot up**
   - Tug resistance indicates roots
   - Remove covering gradually
   - Pot in regular mix

### Air Layering (Higher Success)

**When to use:** For reliable results or leggy plants.

**Process:**
1. Select point on stem
2. Remove small section of bark (ring about 2cm)
3. Apply rooting hormone
4. Wrap with moist sphagnum moss
5. Cover with plastic wrap, secure both ends
6. Keep moss moist
7. Roots visible in 6-10 weeks
8. Cut below roots and plant

## Rooting Environment

| Factor | Requirement |
|--------|-------------|
| **Temperature** | Warm (22-28°C) |
| **Humidity** | High |
| **Light** | Bright indirect |
| **Medium** | Well-draining |
| **Water** | Barely moist |

### Environment Setup

**For cuttings:**
Covered container for humidity, warm position, bright but not direct light. Bottom heat beneficial.

**Critical factor:**
Handle the latex sap. Rinse or let dry before planting. Sap can cause rot if sealed into wound.

## Common Problems & Solutions

### Problem 1: Cutting Rots

**Symptoms:** Stem turns black at base.

**Causes:** Too wet, sap not rinsed, bacterial infection.

**Solution:** Start fresh with new cutting.

**Prevention:** Rinse or dry sap, well-draining medium, barely moist not wet.

### Problem 2: Leaves Drop

**Symptoms:** Leaves fall during rooting.

**Causes:** Stress, low humidity, excessive water loss.

**Solution:** Increase humidity. Continue caring for cutting - it may still root.

**Prevention:** Roll large leaves to reduce water loss, maintain humidity.

### Problem 3: Variegated Cuttings Fail

**Symptoms:** Variegated cultivars struggle more than green types.

**Causes:** Lower chlorophyll = less energy for rooting.

**Solution:** Be more patient, consider air layering.

**Prevention:** Choose cuttings with good balance of green (for energy) and variegation.

## Pro Tips

1. **Roll the leaves** - Large rubber plant leaves lose lots of water. Roll and secure with rubber band to reduce transpiration.

2. **Rinse the sap** - Milky latex can cause rot. Rinse cut end under water or let dry completely before planting.

3. **Air layering is reliable** - Nearly guaranteed success. Worth the effort for single plants.

4. **Warm is essential** - Room temperature isn't enough in winter. Wait for warm weather or provide bottom heat.

5. **Single leaf won't work** - Unlike some plants, single leaves without stem section won't develop into plants.

## Mother Plant Care

### Maintaining Stock Plants

Healthy rubber plants provide good cutting material:

- **Position:** Bright indirect to some direct light.
- **Watering:** Allow to dry somewhat between waterings.
- **Feeding:** Monthly during growing season.
- **Pruning:** Encourages branching; provides cuttings.

### Selecting Material

Choose cuttings that display:

- Healthy, glossy leaves
- Firm stems
- Good coloring (for variegated types)
- No pests or disease
