## Overview

Acacias (wattles) are the largest genus of Australian plants, with over 1,000 species. Most propagate readily from seed, making this the standard method for species wattles.

The key to acacia seed germination is breaking dormancy through heat or scarification. In nature, bushfire provides this; in cultivation, boiling water treatment is the standard approach.

Cutting propagation is possible for some species, particularly smaller shrub wattles, and is necessary for named cultivars that must be maintained vegetatively. However, success varies significantly between species.

## Best Uses for Propagated Plants

- Rapid screening and windbreaks
- Native revegetation
- Feature specimens (selected forms)
- Nitrogen-fixing ground stabilization
- Wildlife habitat

## Propagation Methods

### Seed (Recommended for Most Species)

**Overview:** Reliable method once dormancy is broken.

**When to sow:**
- Best months: September to November (spring)

**Step-by-step process:**

1. **Collect or obtain seed**
   - Mature seed pods
   - Purchase from native seed suppliers
   - Seed remains viable for years (decades!)

2. **Break dormancy** (essential for most species)
   - **Boiling water method:** Pour boiling water over seeds, soak 24 hours
   - **Scarification:** Nick seed coat with knife or sandpaper
   - **Hot water variants:** 80-100°C water depending on species
   - Seeds that swell are viable; discard non-swelling seeds

3. **Sow treated seed**
   - Free-draining native seed mix
   - Plant 1-2cm deep
   - Firm well

4. **Provide conditions**
   - Warm (20-25°C)
   - Keep moist but well-drained
   - Bright light

5. **Germination** (1-4 weeks after treatment)
   - Rapid once dormancy broken
   - Transplant when large enough to handle
   - Handle carefully - don't disturb roots

6. **Grow on**
   - Pot up carefully
   - Don't let become pot-bound
   - Plant out while young

### Cuttings (Some Species)

**When to use:** For specific cultivars or reliable species.

**When to take material:**
- Best months: February to April (late summer to autumn)

**Process:**
1. Select semi-hardwood current growth
2. Take 8-12cm lengths
3. Remove lower phyllodes (leaves)
4. Apply medium-strength rooting hormone
5. Insert in free-draining mix
6. Moderate humidity, warmth
7. Rooting 6-10 weeks
8. Success varies by species

**Species that root reasonably from cuttings:**
- A. cognata (River wattle)
- A. baileyana (Cootamundra wattle)
- A. iteaphylla (Flinders Range wattle)

**Species difficult from cuttings:**
- Most large tree wattles
- Many inland species

## Rooting Environment

| Factor | Requirement |
|--------|-------------|
| **Temperature** | Warm (20-25°C) |
| **Humidity** | Moderate |
| **Light** | Bright |
| **Medium** | Well-drained |

### Environment Setup

**For seed:**
Standard conditions after proper treatment. Free-draining mix, warmth, moisture.

**For cuttings:**
Well-drained medium. Moderate humidity. Bottom heat helpful.

**Critical factor:**
Seed dormancy must be broken. Untreated seed can sit dormant for years.

## Common Problems & Solutions

### Problem 1: Seed Doesn't Germinate

**Symptoms:** Weeks after sowing, no germination.

**Causes:** Dormancy not broken (treatment didn't work or wasn't done).

**Solution:** Re-treat remaining seed with boiling water, longer soaking.

**Prevention:** Always treat acacia seed. Discard seeds that don't swell after treatment.

### Problem 2: Seedlings Damp Off

**Symptoms:** Seedlings collapse at soil level.

**Causes:** Fungal disease from overwatering.

**Solution:** Improve drainage, reduce watering.

**Prevention:** Free-draining mix, don't overwater, good ventilation.

### Problem 3: Cuttings Fail Despite Good Conditions

**Symptoms:** Multiple batches don't root.

**Causes:** Some acacia species are difficult to impossible from cuttings.

**Solution:** Use seed instead.

**Prevention:** Research your specific species. Some simply don't root from cuttings.

## Pro Tips

1. **Boiling water is magic** - The traditional boiling water treatment works for most acacias. Pour boiling water over seeds, leave to soak overnight.

2. **Swelling means viable** - Seeds that swell after treatment are viable. Those that don't swell may need harder treatment or are not viable.

3. **Short-lived but fast** - Many acacias are fast-growing but short-lived (10-20 years). Plan for succession.

4. **Nitrogen fixers** - Acacias fix nitrogen, improving soil for companion plants. Valuable in native plantings.

5. **Seed longevity** - Acacia seed can remain viable for decades if stored dry. Very long-lived seed bank.

## Mother Plant Care

### Maintaining Stock Plants

For seed collection:
- **Timing:** Collect pods when brown but before splitting
- **Storage:** Dry pods, extract seed, store cool and dry
- **Selection:** Collect from healthy, well-formed specimens

For cutting production:
- **Pruning:** Light tip pruning produces cutting material
- **Health:** Vigorous plants provide best material
- **Young plants:** Often root more readily than old
