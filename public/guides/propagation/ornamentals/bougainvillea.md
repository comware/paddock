# Bougainvillea Propagation

## Quick Facts

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Difficulty** | Easy-Intermediate |
| **Best Method** | Semi-hardwood cuttings |
| **Alternative Methods** | Hardwood cuttings, layering |
| **Time to Root** | 4-8 weeks |
| **Best Season** | Spring to summer |
| **Success Rate** | 70-90% typical |
| **Rooting Hormone** | Recommended |

## Overview

Bougainvilleas are surprisingly easy to propagate for such spectacular plants. Their vibrant bracts (the colorful "flowers" are actually modified leaves) and vigorous growth make them popular in warm climates, and cuttings root readily with basic technique.

Semi-hardwood cuttings taken in warm weather give the best results. Warmth accelerates rooting, and bougainvilleas being tropical plants respond well to heat. The thorns can make handling challenging, but the ease of rooting compensates.

Bougainvillea cuttings produce exact clones of the parent, maintaining flower color and growth habit - important given the wide range of colors and forms available.

## Best Uses for Propagated Plants

- Creating colorful screening and hedges
- Container specimens
- Training over pergolas and fences
- Multiplying favorite color forms
- Sharing with other gardeners

## Propagation Methods

### Semi-Hardwood Cuttings (Recommended)

**Overview:** Reliable method with good success rates.

**When to take material:**
- Best months: October to January (spring to midsummer)
- Wood condition: Current season's growth, slightly firmed

**Step-by-step process:**

1. **Select material**
   - Current season's growth, firmed but not woody
   - 15-20cm lengths
   - Pencil-thick or slightly thicker
   - Healthy, pest-free

2. **Prepare cuttings**
   - Cut below a node at base
   - Remove lower leaves
   - Keep 2-3 leaves at top
   - Watch the thorns!

3. **Apply rooting hormone**
   - Medium-strength formula
   - Recommended but not essential
   - Dip base

4. **Insert cuttings**
   - Well-drained propagation mix
   - Insert 5-8cm deep
   - Firm gently

5. **Provide environment**
   - Warm conditions (22-28°C ideal)
   - Moderate to high humidity
   - Bright light (can take some direct sun)
   - Cover or propagator

6. **Ongoing care** (4-8 weeks)
   - Keep moist but well-drained
   - Good ventilation
   - Warmth accelerates rooting

7. **Pot up and grow on**
   - Well-drained potting mix
   - Full sun position
   - Protect from frost initially

### Hardwood Cuttings (Winter Method)

**When to use:** When dormant wood available, or for larger cuttings.

**When to take material:**
- Best months: June to August (winter)

**Process:**
1. Cut 20-30cm lengths of mature wood
2. Remove leaves (will have dropped or can strip)
3. Apply rooting hormone
4. Plant 2/3 depth in ground or pots
5. Keep moist, sheltered position
6. Slower rooting but works

### Layering (Simple Alternative)

**When to use:** For a few plants with minimal effort.

**Process:**
1. Select low branch in spring
2. Wound underside, apply hormone
3. Pin to ground, cover with soil
4. Keep moist
5. Roots in 2-4 months
6. Sever and transplant

## Rooting Environment

| Factor | Requirement |
|--------|-------------|
| **Temperature** | Warm (22-28°C) |
| **Humidity** | Moderate to high |
| **Light** | Bright, some sun okay |
| **Medium** | Well-drained |
| **Bottom heat** | Helpful |

### Environment Setup

**Propagation unit:**
Covered container or propagator. Warm position. Good drainage. Can handle more light than many cuttings.

**Key factor:**
Warmth is the main driver. Bougainvilleas are tropical and root fastest in warm conditions.

## Common Problems & Solutions

### Problem 1: Cuttings Rot Before Rooting

**Symptoms:** Base turns black, cutting collapses.

**Causes:** Too wet, too cold, poor drainage.

**Solution:** Improve drainage, increase warmth, water less.

**Prevention:** Well-drained medium, warm conditions, don't overwater.

### Problem 2: Leaves Drop But Cutting Survives

**Symptoms:** All leaves fall, but stem remains firm and green.

**Causes:** Normal stress response, or humidity too low.

**Solution:** Maintain conditions - can still root successfully.

**Prevention:** Higher humidity reduces leaf drop.

### Problem 3: Rooted Plant Doesn't Flower

**Symptoms:** Healthy plant with good growth but no bracts.

**Causes:** Too much nitrogen, insufficient light, insufficient stress.

**Solution:** Full sun, reduce nitrogen, slight water stress can trigger flowering.

**Prevention:** Bougainvilleas flower best with some stress - don't pamper too much.

## Pro Tips

1. **Heat accelerates everything** - Bougainvilleas are tropical. The warmer the conditions (within reason), the faster they root.

2. **Handle thorns carefully** - Most bougainvilleas have sharp thorns. Thick gloves are recommended when taking and handling cuttings.

3. **Color runs true** - Cuttings produce exact color matches to the parent. Important with the wide range of bougainvillea colors available.

4. **Stress triggers flowering** - Established bougainvilleas flower best with some water stress and full sun. Don't over-fertilize or overwater.

5. **Frost protection needed** - Young plants are frost-tender. Protect first-winter plants from frost damage.

## Mother Plant Care

### Maintaining Stock Plants

Healthy bougainvilleas produce the best cutting material:

- **Position:** Full sun, warm position.
- **Pruning:** Regular pruning provides cutting material and maintains shape.
- **Feeding:** Moderate - excess nitrogen reduces flowering.
- **Watering:** Drought-tolerant once established; regular water for young plants.

### Selecting Material

Choose material that displays:

- Current season's growth, semi-hardened
- Healthy, pest-free condition
- From known flowering plants (color verified)
- Typical growth for the variety
