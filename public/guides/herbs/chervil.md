## Overview

Chervil is one of the classic French fines herbes, with a subtle flavor combining mild anise and parsley notes. As microgreens, chervil produces delicate, lacy leaves with this distinctive gentle flavor.

Chervil prefers cool conditions and bolts quickly in heat, making it a spring/fall crop in most climates. The flavor is delicate and easily overwhelmed, so it's used as a finishing herb rather than a cooking ingredient.

For growers serving French restaurants or fine dining establishments, chervil microgreens offer an authentic ingredient that's difficult to source fresh.

## Best Uses

- French cuisine (fines herbes)
- Egg dishes
- Delicate fish
- Finishing herb
- Fine dining
- Spring dishes

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Chervil seeds (Anthriscus cerefolium)
- Dark, elongated seeds
- Fresh seeds important

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- Use within 1 year (short viability)

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** Yes

Soak 8-12 hours, drain.

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Fine coco coir or soil

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 25-40g per 10x20 tray

Spread evenly, do not cover.

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 4-5 days

Keep COOL - 15-18°C ideal. Chervil hates heat.

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** When germination visible

Moderate light, 12-14 hours. Keep cool.

### 7. Watering

Keep moist. Cool conditions essential.

### 8. Harvest

**Timing:** 14-21 days, height 5-8cm, lacy leaves

**Yield:** 100-200g from 10x20 tray

### 9. Post-Harvest

Very delicate. Refrigerate immediately, 5-7 days shelf life.

## Pro Tips

1. **Cool Essential** - Chervil bolts and becomes bitter in heat. Spring/fall crop only.

2. **French Connection** - One of the fines herbes. Target French restaurants.

3. **Delicate Flavor** - Subtle flavor needs subtle applications. Don't overwhelm.

4. **Fresh Seeds** - Chervil seeds have short viability. Use fresh.

5. **Egg Pairing** - Classic with eggs in French cuisine.
