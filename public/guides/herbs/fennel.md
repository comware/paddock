# Fennel Microgreens

## Quick Facts

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Difficulty** | Intermediate |
| **Days to Harvest** | 14-21 days |
| **Blackout Period** | 4-5 days |
| **Pre-soak Required** | Yes (8-12 hours) |
| **Seed Density** | 25-40g per 10x20 tray |
| **Expected Yield** | 5-7:1 ratio |
| **Flavor Profile** | Sweet anise/licorice flavor, feathery appearance |

## Overview

Fennel microgreens deliver the distinctive sweet anise/licorice flavor of fennel in a delicate, feathery form. Like dill, fennel produces finely divided fronds that are both beautiful and flavorful.

The anise flavor is divisive - some love it, others don't. This makes fennel a specialty item rather than a mass-market product. Those who appreciate fennel flavor find the microgreens to be a perfect expression of it.

Growing is similar to dill - moderate difficulty with slow germination and delicate plants.

## Best Uses

- Fish dishes (especially Mediterranean)
- Italian cuisine
- Pork dishes
- Anise flavor without whole fennel
- Elegant feathery garnish
- Seafood restaurants

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Fennel seeds
- Greenish-brown, ridged, oval seeds
- Fresh seeds for best germination

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- Use within 2 years

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** Yes - helps germination

**Process:**
1. Soak 8-12 hours
2. Drain thoroughly
3. Plant promptly

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Fine coco coir or seed-starting mix

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 25-40g per 10x20 tray

Spread evenly, press gently.

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 4-5 days

Standard conditions, keep moist.

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** When germination visible

Moderate light, 12-14 hours.

### 7. Watering

Keep moist. Gentle watering for delicate plants.

### 8. Harvest

**Timing:** 14-21 days, height 5-8cm, feathery fronds

**Yield:** 150-250g from 10x20 tray

### 9. Post-Harvest

Delicate handling required. Refrigerate, 5-7 days.
