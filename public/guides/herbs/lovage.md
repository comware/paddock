## Overview

Lovage is an underappreciated herb with intense celery-like flavor - sometimes called the "Maggi plant" because its flavor resembles the famous seasoning. As microgreens, lovage delivers concentrated celery essence without growing actual celery (which is extremely difficult as a microgreen).

The flavor is strong and distinctive - a little goes a long way. This makes lovage microgreens ideal for applications where celery flavor is desired without the texture or bulk of celery.

Growing lovage microgreens is challenging due to slow germination and low yields, but the unique flavor profile offers market differentiation.

## Best Uses

- Celery flavor substitute
- Soup and stock seasoning
- Bloody Mary garnish
- European cuisine
- Potato dishes
- Strong flavor applications

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Lovage seeds (Levisticum officinale)
- Similar to celery seeds
- Fresh seeds important

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- Use within 1-2 years

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** Yes - extended soak

Soak 12-24 hours, drain thoroughly.

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Fine coco coir or soil

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 25-40g per 10x20 tray

Spread evenly, do not cover.

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 5-7 days

Keep at 15-20°C, moist. Be patient.

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** When germination visible

Moderate light, 12-14 hours.

### 7. Watering

Keep moist throughout long cycle.

### 8. Harvest

**Timing:** 18-25 days, height 5-10cm, true leaves developing

**Yield:** 100-150g from 10x20 tray (low)

### 9. Post-Harvest

Refrigerate, 7-10 days shelf life. Strong aroma.
