## Overview

Parsley microgreens are notoriously difficult due to extremely slow germination - parsley seeds contain germination inhibitors that require extensive soaking to overcome. The old saying "parsley goes to the devil and back before it germinates" reflects this reality.

When successful, parsley microgreens deliver authentic parsley flavor in miniature form. Both flat-leaf (Italian) and curly varieties work, though flat-leaf is preferred for flavor intensity.

The very long cycle (often 4 weeks) and low germination rates make parsley one of the most challenging microgreens. Only attempt after mastering easier crops.

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Flat-leaf (Italian) parsley preferred for flavor
- Small, curved, ridged seeds
- Fresh seeds critical

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- Use within 1-2 years

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** Yes - extensive soaking critical

**Process:**
1. Soak 24-48 hours (yes, that long)
2. Change water every 12 hours
3. Some growers pour off initial soak water (contains inhibitors)
4. Drain thoroughly before planting

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Fine coco coir or seed-starting mix

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 30-50g per 10x20 tray

Spread evenly, do not cover (needs light to germinate).

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 5-7 days (loose cover, some light OK)

**Note:** Parsley actually needs some light to germinate. Use loose covering.

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** When any germination visible (may take 2+ weeks total)

Moderate light, 12-14 hours.

### 7. Watering

Keep consistently moist. Long cycle means careful moisture management.

### 8. Harvest

**Timing:** 18-28 days, height 5-8cm, true parsley leaf shape visible

**Yield:** 100-200g from 10x20 tray (low)

### 9. Post-Harvest

Handle gently. Refrigerate, 7-10 days shelf life.

## Common Problems & Solutions

### Problem 1: No Germination

**Symptoms:** Nothing happens for weeks

**Causes:** Insufficient soaking, germination inhibitors not washed out

**Solution:** Longer soak with water changes, fresh seeds

**Prevention:** Full 48-hour soak, change water multiple times

### Problem 2: Spotty Germination

**Symptoms:** Uneven germination across tray

**Causes:** Natural parsley characteristic

**Solution:** Accept lower yields, over-seed slightly

**Prevention:** Fresh seeds, thorough soaking

## Pro Tips

1. **Patience Extreme** - Parsley is one of the slowest germinators. 3-4 weeks typical.

2. **Long Soak Critical** - Don't shortcut the 48-hour soak with water changes.

3. **Light for Germination** - Unlike most seeds, parsley benefits from light during germination.

4. **Premium Pricing** - Difficulty and low yield justify premium prices.

5. **Fresh Seeds** - Old parsley seeds may simply not germinate at all.
