## Overview

Dill microgreens capture the fresh, aromatic flavor of dill in a delicate miniature form. The feathery leaves are visually beautiful and deliver authentic dill flavor that's prized in Scandinavian, Eastern European, and Mediterranean cuisines.

Dill is moderately challenging - seeds are slow to germinate and plants are delicate. The feathery fronds can tangle, making harvesting tricky. But the unique flavor and beautiful appearance make dill worth the effort.

Dill microgreens pair naturally with fish, cucumbers, and cream-based dishes.

## Best Uses

- Fish dishes (especially salmon)
- Scandinavian cuisine
- Cucumber and yogurt dishes
- Cream cheese topping
- Eastern European cuisine
- Elegant feathery garnish

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Dill seeds
- Flat, oval, ridged seeds
- Fresh seeds for better germination

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- Use within 2 years

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** Optional but helpful

Soak 4-8 hours to speed germination.

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Fine coco coir or seed-starting mix

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 25-40g per 10x20 tray

Spread evenly, press lightly, do not cover (needs light).

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 4-5 days (minimal - dill likes light)

Light covering, some light can penetrate.

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** Early - when germination begins

Moderate to bright light, 12-14 hours.

### 7. Watering

Keep moist. Gentle misting - delicate plants.

### 8. Harvest

**Timing:** 14-21 days, height 5-8cm, feathery fronds developed

**Yield:** 150-250g from 10x20 tray

### 9. Post-Harvest

Very delicate - handle carefully. Tangled fronds can be tricky. Refrigerate, 5-7 days.

## Common Problems & Solutions

### Problem 1: Slow Germination

**Symptoms:** Takes 7+ days to germinate

**Causes:** Dill is naturally slow

**Solution:** Pre-soak seeds, be patient

**Prevention:** Accept dill's slower pace

### Problem 2: Tangled Harvest

**Symptoms:** Feathery fronds tangle together

**Causes:** Nature of dill's growth

**Solution:** Harvest in small sections

**Prevention:** Some tangling is inevitable
