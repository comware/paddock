# Carrot Microgreens

## Quick Facts

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Difficulty** | Advanced |
| **Days to Harvest** | 18-25 days |
| **Blackout Period** | 5-7 days |
| **Pre-soak Required** | Yes (12-24 hours) |
| **Seed Density** | 15-20g per 10x20 tray |
| **Expected Yield** | 3-5:1 ratio |
| **Flavor Profile** | Mild carrot flavor, earthy, fresh with feathery appearance |

## Overview

Carrot microgreens offer a delicate carrot flavor in an elegant, feathery form. The thin, wispy leaves look almost like dill but taste distinctly of carrot. The flavor is subtle - milder than mature carrots but with that unmistakable earthy-sweet carrot character.

Like celery, carrot microgreens are classified as advanced due to very slow germination, small yields, and longer growing times. Carrot seeds are also small and can be tricky to spread evenly.

The visual appeal of carrot microgreens - that delicate, feathery appearance - makes them prized for garnishing and adding visual interest to plates.

## Best Uses

- Elegant garnish (primary use)
- Light soups and consommé
- Carrot dishes (reinforcing flavor)
- Delicate salads
- Photography and plating

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Carrot seeds (small, brown, ribbed)
- Any carrot variety works
- Fresh seeds important

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- Sealed container
- Use within 1-2 years

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** Yes - essential for slow-germinating carrots

**Process:**
1. Measure 15-20g
2. Soak in room temperature water 12-24 hours
3. Drain through fine mesh strainer
4. Some growers add a brief refrigerator soak (stratification)

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Fine coco coir or seed-starting mix

**Setup:**
1. 1-2cm of fine, level medium
2. Pre-moisten thoroughly
3. Smooth surface for small seeds

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 15-20g per 10x20 tray

**Technique:**
1. Spread soaked seeds as evenly as possible
2. Seeds may clump - do your best
3. Press very lightly into surface
4. Do NOT cover with medium
5. Mist gently

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 5-7 days (slow germinator)

**Method:**
1. Cover loosely
2. Keep at 15-20°C (cooler is better for carrots)
3. Mist daily - maintain moisture
4. Check for germination progress

**What to expect:**
- Day 1-4: Little visible activity
- Day 5-7: Tiny sprouts begin emerging
- Germination continues unevenly

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** When first sprouts are clearly visible

**Light requirements:**
- Moderate light
- 12-14 hours per day
- Carrots tolerate lower light than some crops

**What happens:**
- Slow greening
- Feathery leaves begin developing
- Distinctive carrot appearance emerges

### 7. Watering

**Method:** Gentle misting or bottom watering

**Frequency:**
- Keep consistently moist
- Don't let dry out
- Avoid overwatering - balance needed

### 8. Harvest

**Timing indicators:**
- 18-25 days from seeding
- Height: 5-8cm
- Feathery leaves well developed
- Carrot aroma when touched

**Technique:**
1. Cut just above medium
2. Harvest in morning
3. Very gentle handling - leaves are delicate

**Yield:** 50-100g from 10x20 tray (low yield)

### 9. Post-Harvest

**Handling:**
1. Extremely gentle - leaves break easily
2. Minimal washing
3. Handle by stem bases when possible

**Storage:**
- Refrigerate immediately
- Shelf life: 5-7 days
- Store dry - moisture damages delicate leaves

## Common Problems & Solutions

### Problem 1: Very Poor Germination

**Symptoms:** Few or no seeds germinate

**Causes:** Old seeds, too warm, insufficient soak

**Solution:** Fresh seeds, cooler temps, longer soak

**Prevention:** Seeds under 2 years, 15-20°C, 24 hour soak

### Problem 2: Weak, Pale Greens

**Symptoms:** Thin, yellowish growth

**Causes:** Insufficient light, poor quality medium

**Solution:** More light exposure, fresh medium

**Prevention:** Adequate lighting, quality supplies

### Problem 3: Damping Off

**Symptoms:** Seedlings collapse

**Causes:** Overwatering, poor drainage

**Solution:** Reduce watering, improve airflow

**Prevention:** Balance moisture, good drainage

## Pro Tips

1. **Cool Germination** - Carrots germinate better in cooler conditions. Don't try to warm them up.

2. **Visual Focus** - The feathery appearance is the main selling point. Market for garnishing.

3. **Pair with Root Dishes** - Carrot microgreens on dishes featuring root vegetables creates a compelling story.

4. **Low Yield Reality** - Accept that yield will be low. Price to compensate.

5. **Patience is Everything** - Carrot microgreens require patience. They'll get there eventually.

6. **Fine Dining Focus** - Target high-end restaurants that appreciate delicate presentations.

## Scheduling Recommendations

- **Limited production:** Keep volume small due to difficulty
- **Specialty crop:** Grow for specific customers
- **Long cycle:** Plan 4 weeks from seed to harvest

- Premium garnish market
- Fine dining focus
