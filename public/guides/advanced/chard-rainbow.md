# Chard (Rainbow/Swiss) Microgreens

## Quick Facts

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Difficulty** | Advanced |
| **Days to Harvest** | 12-18 days |
| **Blackout Period** | 4-5 days |
| **Pre-soak Required** | Yes (8-12 hours) |
| **Seed Density** | 50-70g per 10x20 tray |
| **Expected Yield** | 5-7:1 ratio |
| **Flavor Profile** | Mild, slightly earthy, similar to beet but milder with multicolored stems |

## Overview

Rainbow chard (Swiss chard) microgreens produce a vibrant mix of stem colors - red, pink, orange, yellow, and white - all in the same tray. The visual effect is stunning, making rainbow chard a favorite for colorful presentations.

Chard is closely related to beets (same species, Beta vulgaris), so it shares many growing characteristics including the multigerm seed structure that produces clumpy growth. The flavor is similar to beets but milder and less sweet.

Rainbow chard microgreens are classified as advanced due to the same challenges as beets - multigerm seeds, clumpy growth, and susceptibility to damping off.

## Best Uses

- Colorful salads (primary use)
- Visual garnish
- Mixed microgreen trays
- Rainbow presentations
- Photography and plating

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Rainbow chard or Bright Lights variety
- Contains mix of colors (genetics vary)
- Same multigerm structure as beets

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- Sealed container
- 3-4 years viability

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** Yes - same as beets

**Process:**
1. Measure 50-70g
2. Soak 8-12 hours
3. Change water once if possible
4. Drain and rinse

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Coco coir or soil with good drainage

**Setup:** 2-3cm moistened medium, level surface.

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 50-70g per 10x20 tray

**Technique:**
Same as beets - spread evenly, expect multigerm clumping.

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 4-5 days

**Method:**
1. Cover with inverted tray
2. Light weight (1-2kg)
3. Keep at 18-22°C
4. Mist daily

**What to expect:**
- Day 3-4: Multiple stems per cluster
- Day 4-5: First hints of color in stems

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** When stems 2-3cm with cotyledons forming

**Light requirements:**
- Bright light develops best colors
- 12-16 hours per day
- Even lighting for consistent color development

**What happens:**
- Colors develop and intensify
- Range of red, pink, orange, yellow, white stems
- Cotyledons green up

### 7. Watering

**Method:** Bottom watering preferred

**Frequency:**
- Once or twice daily
- Keep moist but not wet
- Same rot risk as beets

### 8. Harvest

**Timing indicators:**
- 12-18 days from seeding
- Height: 5-8cm
- Full color development
- Mix of stem colors visible

**Technique:**
1. Cut above medium
2. Harvest clumps together
3. Aim for good color mix in each harvest portion

**Yield:** 250-400g from 10x20 tray

### 9. Post-Harvest

**Handling:**
1. Handle gently
2. Quick rinse if needed
3. Arrange to showcase color variety

**Storage:**
- Refrigerate immediately
- Shelf life: 7-10 days
- Colors hold well

## Common Problems & Solutions

Same challenges as beet microgreens - multigerm clumping, rot risk, and color development requiring adequate light.

### Problem: Uneven Color Mix

**Symptoms:** Some trays mostly one color, not rainbow effect

**Causes:** Genetic variation in seed batch, uneven lighting

**Solution:** Source seeds from different suppliers, ensure even lighting

**Prevention:** Quality rainbow chard seed, rotate trays

---

## Pro Tips

1. **Seed Quality Varies** - Rainbow mix quality depends on seed source. Some batches are more colorful than others.

2. **Light for Color** - Like all colored microgreens, light intensity determines color vibrancy.

3. **Color Showcase** - Arrange harvested greens to display full color range. The rainbow effect is the selling point.

4. **Mix Consistency** - Each harvest should have a good color mix. Check before packaging.

5. **Beet Alternative** - For those who want color without the beet flavor, chard is milder.

6. **Visual Priority** - Flavor is secondary to visual appeal for rainbow chard. Market accordingly.

---

## Scheduling Recommendations

- **Continuous supply:** Plant every 5-7 days
- **Visual product:** Quality check each harvest for good color mix
- **Year-round:** No seasonal limitations with proper lighting

## Color Genetics

Rainbow chard produces these stem colors:
- Red/Pink
- Orange
- Yellow/Gold
- White/Cream

The ratio varies by seed batch and growing conditions.
