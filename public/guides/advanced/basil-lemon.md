# Basil (Lemon) Microgreens

## Quick Facts

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Difficulty** | Advanced |
| **Days to Harvest** | 12-18 days |
| **Blackout Period** | 4-5 days |
| **Pre-soak Required** | No (mucilaginous seeds) |
| **Seed Density** | 20-30g per 10x20 tray |
| **Expected Yield** | 5-7:1 ratio |
| **Flavor Profile** | Citrus-forward basil with bright lemon notes |

## Overview

Lemon basil microgreens offer a delightful citrus twist on traditional basil flavor. The lemon notes are clear and refreshing, making these microgreens particularly versatile in dishes where a bright, fresh accent is desired.

Growing requirements are identical to other basil varieties - warmth, conservative watering, and no pre-soaking of the mucilaginous seeds. Lemon basil may be slightly more vigorous than Genovese in some conditions.

The citrus character makes lemon basil popular for seafood dishes, desserts, and beverages - applications where traditional basil might not fit.

## Best Uses

- Seafood and fish dishes
- Light salads
- Desserts and fruit dishes
- Cocktails and beverages
- Asian fusion cuisine

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Lemon basil (Ocimum citriodorum)
- Small, dark seeds
- Fresh seeds for best germination

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- Use within 2 years

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** No - never soak basil seeds

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Fine coco coir or soil with excellent drainage

**Setup:** 1-2cm moistened medium, level surface.

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 20-30g per 10x20 tray

Same technique as other basil varieties - sprinkle dry, mist gently.

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 4-5 days

Same protocol as Genovese - warmth (21-25°C), light misting, good airflow.

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** Cotyledons forming

**Light requirements:**
- Moderate to bright light
- 12-16 hours per day

### 7. Watering

**Method:** Bottom watering essential

Conservative approach - basil hates wet feet.

### 8. Harvest

**Timing indicators:**
- 12-18 days from seeding
- Height: 4-6cm
- Citrus aroma when leaves touched

**Yield:** 100-200g from 10x20 tray

### 9. Post-Harvest

**Storage:**
- Refrigerate around 10-12°C
- Shelf life: 3-5 days
- Best fresh

## Pro Tips

1. **Seafood Perfect** - Market specifically for fish dishes. The lemon notes complement seafood beautifully.

2. **Dessert Potential** - Unique among microgreens for dessert applications. Fruit salads, ice cream.

3. **Cocktail Garnish** - Bars pay well for unique garnishes. Lemon basil in drinks is impressive.

4. **Same Care as Genovese** - Grow all three basil varieties together for efficient production.

5. **Aroma is Everything** - If you don't smell citrus, conditions need improvement.
