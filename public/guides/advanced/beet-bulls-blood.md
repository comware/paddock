## Overview

Bull's Blood beet microgreens offer the deepest, most dramatic color of any beet variety. The stems and even the undersides of cotyledons develop an intense burgundy/purple color that makes them a favorite for visual presentations.

The growing requirements are identical to Detroit Dark Red beets - same multigerm seed structure, same soaking requirements, same challenges with clumpy growth. The main difference is the more intense coloring that Bull's Blood develops.

Bull's Blood is often the preferred beet variety for microgreen growers due to its superior visual impact.

## Best Uses

- High-impact garnish
- Color contrast in salads
- Premium plating
- Photography and food styling
- Any application needing dramatic color

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Bull's Blood beet variety specifically
- Same multigerm structure as other beets
- Deep color genetics

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- Sealed container
- 3-4 years viability

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** Yes - same as Detroit Dark Red

**Process:**
1. Soak 50-70g for 8-12 hours
2. Change water once if possible
3. Drain and rinse

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Coco coir or soil with good drainage

**Setup:** 2-3cm moistened medium, level surface.

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 50-70g per 10x20 tray

Same technique as Detroit - spread evenly, expect clumping.

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 4-5 days

Same protocol as Detroit. Purple coloring may be visible earlier.

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** When stems showing color at 2-3cm

**Light requirements:**
- Bright light is CRITICAL for Bull's Blood
- 12-16 hours per day
- More light = deeper purple

**What happens:**
- Dramatic color development
- Deep burgundy throughout
- Some cotyledons develop purple undersides

### 7. Watering

**Method:** Bottom watering preferred

Same as Detroit - moist but not wet, prone to rot.

### 8. Harvest

**Timing indicators:**
- 12-18 days from seeding
- Maximum color development
- Deep burgundy stems throughout

**Yield:** 250-400g from 10x20 tray

### 9. Post-Harvest

**Storage:**
- Refrigerate immediately
- Shelf life: 7-10 days
- Color holds exceptionally well

## Common Problems & Solutions

Same as Detroit Dark Red, with one addition:

### Problem: Not Enough Purple

**Symptoms:** Color more red than burgundy, not as dramatic as expected

**Causes:** Insufficient light is the primary cause

**Solution:** Move closer to lights, increase duration

**Prevention:** Bright light from day 1 of light phase

---

## Pro Tips

1. **Light is Non-Negotiable** - Bull's Blood needs bright light to develop its signature deep purple. Don't compromise.

2. **Premium Variety** - Bull's Blood commands higher prices than Detroit due to color. Market accordingly.

3. **Photography Star** - Food photographers love Bull's Blood. Build relationships with food stylists.

4. **Color Consistency** - Even lighting produces even coloring. Rotate trays if using natural light.

5. **Side-by-Side Comparison** - Grow both Detroit and Bull's Blood to offer customers choice.
