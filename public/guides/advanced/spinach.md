# Spinach Microgreens

| **Blackout Period** | 4-5 days |

## Overview

Spinach microgreens deliver classic spinach flavor in a tender, easy-to-use format. The flavor is milder and less oxalic (less of that tooth-coating sensation) than mature spinach, making them more approachable for some palates.

Spinach is classified as advanced due to its temperature sensitivity and susceptibility to bolting (becoming bitter) in warm conditions. It's a cool-season crop that struggles in heat. The seeds can also be slow and uneven germinators.

When grown successfully, spinach microgreens are highly versatile - usable in almost any dish where spinach is appropriate.

## Best Uses

- Salads (primary use)
- Smoothies
- Sandwiches and wraps
- Eggs and omelets
- Light cooking (wilts quickly)

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Any spinach variety works
- Fresh seeds important (spinach loses viability)
- Round or flat seeds depending on variety

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location (refrigeration helps)
- Sealed container
- Use within 1-2 years

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** Yes - improves germination significantly

**Process:**
1. Measure 40-50g
2. Soak in cool water 8-12 hours
3. Some growers use refrigerated soak for better results
4. Drain and rinse
5. Seeds may have residue - normal

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Coco coir or soil - good moisture retention important

**Setup:**
1. 2-3cm of medium
2. Pre-moisten well
3. Level surface

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 40-50g per 10x20 tray

**Technique:**
1. Spread soaked seeds evenly
2. Seeds should be close but not piled
3. Press gently into medium
4. Mist surface
5. Do NOT cover with medium

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 4-5 days

**Method:**
1. Cover with inverted tray
2. Light weight (1kg) can help
3. Keep COOL - 15-18°C ideal
4. Mist daily

**What to expect:**
- Day 1-2: Seeds swell
- Day 3-4: Roots and stems emerge
- Day 4-5: Cotyledons forming

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** When cotyledons are forming

**Light requirements:**
- Moderate light is sufficient
- 12-14 hours per day
- Avoid heat from lights - spinach hates heat

**What happens:**
- Cotyledons green up nicely
- Steady but not explosive growth
- Watch for bolting signs if too warm

### 7. Watering

**Method:** Bottom watering preferred

**Frequency:**
- Once or twice daily
- Keep consistently moist
- Spinach doesn't tolerate drying out well

### 8. Harvest

**Timing indicators:**
- 12-18 days from seeding
- Height: 4-6cm
- Cotyledons fully developed
- Before any stretching or bolting

**Technique:**
1. Cut just above medium
2. Harvest in morning for best texture
3. Harvest promptly - spinach goes downhill quickly once ready

**Yield:** 150-250g from 10x20 tray

### 9. Post-Harvest

**Handling:**
1. Gentle washing
2. Dry thoroughly
3. Handle carefully - bruises easily

**Storage:**
- Refrigerate immediately
- Shelf life: 5-7 days
- Best used fresh

## Common Problems & Solutions

### Problem 1: Poor Germination

**Symptoms:** Patchy, uneven growth

**Causes:** Old seeds, too warm, insufficient soak

**Solution:** Fresh seeds, cooler temps, longer soak

**Prevention:** Seeds under 1 year, cool conditions

### Problem 2: Bolting/Bitterness

**Symptoms:** Stretched growth, bitter flavor

**Causes:** Too warm, stressed

**Solution:** Cool down, harvest immediately

**Prevention:** Keep cool (15-18°C), don't overgrow

### Problem 3: Yellowing

**Symptoms:** Leaves turn yellow

**Causes:** Too warm, overwatered, nutrient deficiency

**Solution:** Cool down, check drainage, fresh medium

**Prevention:** Optimal conditions throughout

### Problem 4: Damping Off

**Symptoms:** Seedlings collapse at base

**Causes:** Overwatering, poor drainage

**Solution:** Reduce watering, improve airflow

**Prevention:** Good drainage, moderate watering

## Pro Tips

1. **Cool is Critical** - Spinach microgreens want to be cool. Heat causes problems at every stage.

2. **Refrigerate Seeds** - Storing seeds in the refrigerator extends their viability.

3. **Quick Turnaround** - Once ready, spinach should be harvested promptly. It doesn't wait.

4. **Summer Challenge** - Consider pausing spinach in hot weather or growing in cooled space.

5. **Universal Appeal** - Almost everyone knows and likes spinach. Easy to market.

6. **Smoothie Market** - Health-conscious consumers love spinach in smoothies. Market this use.

- **Seasonal considerations:** Easier in cool weather
- **Summer strategy:** Grow in coolest available space
