## Overview

Thai basil microgreens offer a distinctly different flavor from Italian basil - strong anise (licorice) notes combined with a spicy, more peppery basil character. The flavor is essential for authentic Thai, Vietnamese, and other Southeast Asian dishes.

Growing requirements are similar to Genovese basil - warmth is essential, overwatering is deadly, and mucilaginous seeds shouldn't be soaked. Thai basil tends to have slightly purple stems, adding visual interest.

Thai basil holds up better to heat in cooking than Italian basil, which is why it's traditionally added to hot dishes like curries and stir-fries.

## Best Uses

- Thai curries (green, red, massaman)
- Pho and Vietnamese soups
- Stir-fries
- Thai basil chicken (pad krapao)
- Southeast Asian cuisine broadly

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Thai basil (Ocimum basilicum var. thyrsiflora)
- Small, dark seeds similar to Genovese
- Fresh seeds important for germination

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- Sealed container
- Use within 2 years

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** No - never soak basil seeds

Seeds are mucilaginous and will clump into unusable mass.

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Fine coco coir or soil with excellent drainage

**Setup:** 1-2cm moistened medium, level surface, good drainage holes.

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 20-30g per 10x20 tray

**Technique:**
1. Sprinkle dry seeds evenly
2. Use shaker for distribution
3. Mist gently - gel forms immediately
4. Do not cover with medium

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 4-5 days

**Method:**
1. Cover loosely
2. Keep at 21-25°C (warmth essential)
3. Mist lightly daily
4. Watch for mold

**What to expect:**
- Similar progression to Genovese basil
- May show purple tinge in stems earlier

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** Cotyledons forming (day 4-5)

**Light requirements:**
- Moderate to bright light
- 12-16 hours per day
- Maintain warmth

**What happens:**
- Distinctive purple coloring may develop in stems
- Strong anise aroma becomes noticeable
- Cotyledons expand

### 7. Watering

**Method:** Bottom watering essential

**Frequency:**
- Once daily
- Conservative watering
- Better dry than wet

### 8. Harvest

**Timing indicators:**
- 12-18 days from seeding
- Height: 4-6cm
- Strong anise aroma
- Purple stem coloring visible

**Technique:**
1. Cut above medium
2. Handle gently
3. Harvest in morning

**Yield:** 100-200g from 10x20 tray

### 9. Post-Harvest

**Handling:**
1. Minimal handling
2. Avoid washing if possible
3. Use quickly

**Storage:**
- Refrigerate around 10-12°C
- Shelf life: 3-5 days
- Best used fresh

## Common Problems & Solutions

Same as Genovese basil - damping off from overwatering is primary concern.

### Problem 1: Weak Anise Flavor

**Symptoms:** Tastes more like regular basil

**Causes:** Insufficient light, too cool, wrong variety

**Solution:** More light, warmer temps, verify seed variety

**Prevention:** Optimal conditions, quality Thai basil seeds
