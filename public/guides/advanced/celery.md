# Celery Microgreens

## Quick Facts

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Difficulty** | Advanced |
| **Days to Harvest** | 18-25 days |
| **Blackout Period** | 5-7 days |
| **Pre-soak Required** | Yes (12-24 hours) |
| **Seed Density** | 10-15g per 10x20 tray |
| **Expected Yield** | 3-5:1 ratio |
| **Flavor Profile** | Intense celery flavor, concentrated and aromatic |

## Overview

Celery microgreens deliver concentrated celery flavor in a tiny package. The flavor is more intense than mature celery stalks - bright, aromatic, and unmistakably celery. A small amount goes a long way.

This is one of the more challenging microgreens due to very slow germination (can take 2-3 weeks just to germinate), tiny seeds that are difficult to handle, and lower yields. However, the concentrated flavor and specialty appeal justify the effort for experienced growers.

Celery microgreens are valued for adding celery flavor without the bulk and moisture of mature celery.

## Best Uses

- Bloody Marys (perfect garnish)
- Soups and stocks
- Potato salad and coleslaw
- Dips and spreads
- Any dish wanting celery flavor

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Celery seeds (tiny, brown, aromatic)
- Fresh seeds - celery loses viability
- Untreated seeds for eating

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry, dark location
- Sealed container
- Use within 1-2 years

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** Yes - helps slow germination

**Process:**
1. Measure 10-15g (seeds are very tiny)
2. Soak in room temperature water 12-24 hours
3. Seeds may clump - this is normal
4. Drain through fine mesh strainer
5. May need to mix with sand for easier spreading

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Very fine coco coir or seed-starting mix

**Setup:**
1. 1-2cm of fine, level medium
2. Pre-moisten well
3. Perfectly smooth surface essential for tiny seeds

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 10-15g per 10x20 tray (tiny seeds)

**Technique:**
1. Mix soaked seeds with fine sand for easier distribution
2. Sprinkle very evenly
3. Do NOT cover - celery needs light to germinate
4. Mist very gently

**Note:** Seeds are tiny and stick together. Patience required.

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 5-7 days (very slow)

**Method:**
1. Cover loosely - some light actually helps celery germinate
2. Keep at 15-20°C (cooler than basil)
3. Mist gently daily - don't wash away seeds
4. Extreme patience required

**What to expect:**
- Day 1-4: Very little visible activity
- Day 5-7: First tiny sprouts visible
- Germination continues to be uneven for days

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** When first sprouts are visible

**Light requirements:**
- Moderate light
- 12-14 hours per day
- Don't stress with intense light

**What happens:**
- Very gradual greening
- Slow but steady growth
- Celery aroma develops

### 7. Watering

**Method:** Very gentle misting or bottom watering

**Frequency:**
- Keep consistently moist
- Celery doesn't tolerate drying out
- But also prone to damping off - balance is key

### 8. Harvest

**Timing indicators:**
- 18-25 days from seeding (very long cycle)
- Height: 4-6cm
- Strong celery aroma
- Thin but established stems

**Technique:**
1. Cut just above medium
2. May need to harvest progressively due to uneven germination
3. Handle gently - very delicate

**Yield:** 40-75g from 10x20 tray (low yield)

### 9. Post-Harvest

**Handling:**
1. Extremely gentle handling
2. Minimal washing
3. Dry carefully

**Storage:**
- Refrigerate immediately
- Shelf life: 5-7 days
- Best used fresh for strongest aroma

## Common Problems & Solutions

### Problem 1: No Germination / Very Poor Germination

**Symptoms:** Seeds never emerge

**Causes:** Old seeds, covered with medium, too warm

**Solution:** Fresh seeds, surface sow, cool conditions

**Prevention:** Seeds less than 1 year old, never cover, 15-20°C

### Problem 2: Seeds Wash Away

**Symptoms:** Seeds clump or disappear after watering

**Causes:** Watering too heavily, seeds not settled

**Solution:** Gentler watering, let seeds adhere before heavy watering

**Prevention:** Light misting only early on

### Problem 3: Extremely Slow Growth

**Symptoms:** Growing but taking forever

**Causes:** Normal for celery - it's slow!

**Solution:** Patience. Celery takes 3+ weeks.

**Prevention:** Plan schedule accordingly

## Pro Tips

1. **Sand Mixing** - Mix seeds with fine sand (3:1 sand to seed) for much easier distribution.

2. **Light Helps Germination** - Unlike many seeds, celery germinates better with some light. Don't use solid blackout.

3. **Bloody Mary Market** - This is the killer application. Bars pay premium for authentic garnish.

4. **Concentrated Flavor** - Use sparingly. A little delivers big celery impact.

5. **Long Commitment** - Plan for 3-4 weeks from seed to harvest. Not a quick crop.

6. **Lower Your Expectations** - Yield will be lower than most microgreens. Price accordingly.
