## Overview

Onion microgreens are thin, elegant shoots with mild onion flavor - all the onion taste without the harsh bite of raw onion. The delicate green stems are beautiful as garnish and functional as flavoring.

The major challenge is time - onions are among the slowest microgreens, taking 3+ weeks to reach harvest. This long cycle requires patience and careful moisture management to avoid problems.

When successful, onion microgreens offer unique flavor that no other microgreen can replicate, commanding premium prices from chefs who value the delicate onion essence.

## Best Uses

- Elegant garnish
- Onion flavor without harsh bite
- Asian cuisine
- Fine dining
- Soup and salad topping
- Photography subject (beautiful thin shoots)

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Onion seeds (any variety)
- Black, angular seeds
- Fresh seeds important (short viability)

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- Use within 1 year for best results

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** Yes - helps slow germination

**Process:**
1. Soak 8-12 hours
2. Drain thoroughly
3. Plant promptly

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Fine coco coir or seed-starting mix

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 40-60g per 10x20 tray

Spread evenly, press gently. Do not cover.

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 5-7 days (long!)

Keep at 18-22°C, mist gently, be patient.

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** When shoots emerge (may take a week)

Moderate light, 12-14 hours daily.

### 7. Watering

Keep moist but not wet. Bottom watering preferred. Don't let dry out.

### 8. Harvest

**Timing:** 18-25 days, height 10-15cm, bright green color

**Yield:** 150-300g from 10x20 tray (low yield)

### 9. Post-Harvest

Very delicate - handle carefully. Refrigerate, 7-10 days shelf life.

## Common Problems & Solutions

### Problem 1: No Germination

**Symptoms:** Seeds don't sprout

**Causes:** Old seeds, insufficient soaking

**Solution:** Fresh seeds, longer soak

**Prevention:** Seeds under 1 year old

### Problem 2: Mold During Long Cycle

**Symptoms:** Mold develops over 3+ weeks

**Causes:** Excessive moisture over time

**Solution:** Improve airflow, reduce watering

**Prevention:** Careful moisture management

## Pro Tips

1. **Patience Required** - 3+ weeks is a long cycle. Plan accordingly.

2. **Fresh Seeds Critical** - Onion seeds lose viability quickly. Use fresh.

3. **Premium Pricing** - Long cycle and low yield justify premium prices.

4. **Unique Flavor** - Nothing else tastes like onion microgreens. Market this uniqueness.

5. **Beautiful Garnish** - Thin green shoots are visually elegant.
