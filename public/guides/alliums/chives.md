## Overview

Chive microgreens are among the slowest and most challenging microgreens to grow - but the reward is genuine chive flavor in a miniature form. The thin, grass-like shoots have the unmistakable taste of chives.

The extremely slow germination and growth (often 4+ weeks from seed to harvest) make chives a specialty crop for dedicated growers. Seeds require extensive pre-soaking and very patient germination.

Successful chive microgreens are rare and command premium prices from chefs who appreciate the authentic chive flavor.

## Best Uses

- Premium garnish
- Authentic chive flavor
- Potato and egg dishes
- Cream cheese and sour cream topping
- Fine dining
- French cuisine

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Chive seeds (Allium schoenoprasum)
- Small, black seeds
- Fresh is critical - poor viability

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- Use within 1 year maximum

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** Yes - extended soak essential

**Process:**
1. Soak 12-24 hours
2. Change water once if possible
3. Drain thoroughly
4. Seeds are very slow to hydrate

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Very fine coco coir or seed-starting mix

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 30-50g per 10x20 tray

Spread evenly, press lightly, do not cover.

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 7-10 days (very long)

**Critical:**
- Keep at 18-22°C
- Mist very gently
- Check for mold during long wait
- Be extremely patient

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** When any green shoots visible

Moderate light, 12-14 hours daily.

### 7. Watering

Keep moist but not wet. Very long cycle means mold risk - careful moisture management.

### 8. Harvest

**Timing:** 21-28 days, height 8-12cm, thin grass-like shoots

**Yield:** 100-200g from 10x20 tray (very low yield)

### 9. Post-Harvest

Very delicate. Refrigerate, 7-10 days shelf life.

## Common Problems & Solutions

### Problem 1: No Germination

**Symptoms:** Nothing happens for weeks

**Causes:** Old seeds, insufficient soaking

**Solution:** Fresh seeds, 24-hour soak

**Prevention:** Use seeds under 1 year old

### Problem 2: Mold During Long Cycle

**Symptoms:** Mold develops before harvest

**Causes:** Too wet over extended period

**Solution:** Improve airflow, reduce moisture

**Prevention:** Careful watering, good ventilation

## Pro Tips

1. **Extreme Patience** - 4+ weeks is normal. This is not a beginner crop.

2. **Fresh Seeds Only** - Old chive seeds simply won't germinate.

3. **Premium Pricing** - Low yield and long cycle justify very premium pricing.

4. **True Chive Flavor** - Nothing else replicates this. Market the authenticity.

5. **Consider Cost-Benefit** - With 4-week cycle and low yield, economics are challenging.
