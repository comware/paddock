# Leek Microgreens

## Quick Facts

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Difficulty** | Advanced |
| **Days to Harvest** | 18-25 days |
| **Blackout Period** | 5-7 days |
| **Pre-soak Required** | Yes (8-12 hours) |
| **Seed Density** | 40-60g per 10x20 tray |
| **Expected Yield** | 4-6:1 ratio |
| **Flavor Profile** | Mild, sweet onion-like with subtle leek character |

## Overview

Leek microgreens are essentially identical to onion microgreens in growing requirements - slow germination, long cycle, and delicate thin shoots. The flavor is milder and sweeter than onion, with the subtle leek character that makes mature leeks prized in French cuisine.

Growing leeks as microgreens requires the same patience as onions. The 3+ week cycle tests grower commitment but rewards with unique flavor that chefs appreciate.

Leek microgreens pair naturally with potato dishes, soups, and anywhere the subtle allium flavor enhances without overpowering.

## Best Uses

- Potato dish garnish
- Soup topping
- Elegant garnish

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Leek seeds
- Similar to onion - black, angular
- Fresh seeds essential

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- Use within 1 year

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** Yes

Soak 8-12 hours, drain, plant promptly.

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Fine coco coir or soil

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 40-60g per 10x20 tray

Same as onion - spread evenly, don't cover.

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 5-7 days

Standard allium protocol - cool, moist, patient.

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** When shoots emerge

Moderate light, 12-14 hours.

### 7. Watering

Keep moist throughout long cycle. Bottom watering preferred.

### 8. Harvest

**Timing:** 18-25 days, height 10-15cm

**Yield:** 150-300g from 10x20 tray

### 9. Post-Harvest

Delicate. Refrigerate, 7-10 days shelf life.
