# Orach (Mountain Spinach) Microgreens

## Quick Facts

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Difficulty** | Intermediate |
| **Days to Harvest** | 12-16 days |
| **Blackout Period** | 4-5 days |
| **Pre-soak Required** | Optional (4-8 hours) |
| **Seed Density** | 25-35g per 10x20 tray |
| **Expected Yield** | 5-7:1 ratio |
| **Flavor Profile** | Mild, spinach-like with striking red/purple varieties |

## Overview

Orach (also called mountain spinach or French spinach) produces microgreens with a mild, spinach-like flavor. The red and purple varieties are particularly striking, producing deep magenta to purple leaves that add dramatic color to dishes.

Unlike amaranth, orach is easier to grow with larger seeds and more vigorous growth. It's a good alternative to red amaranth for growers who want color but find amaranth too challenging.

Orach tolerates heat better than spinach, making it a useful summer alternative for spinach-like flavor.

## Best Uses

- Colorful salad component (red/purple varieties)
- Spinach alternative (especially in summer)
- Visual garnish
- Mild-flavored colored green

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Red orach or purple orach for color
- Green orach for spinach-like flavor without color
- Larger seeds than amaranth, papery covering

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- 2-3 years viability

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** Optional - 4-8 hour soak can help germination

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Coco coir or soil - not as demanding as amaranth

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 25-35g per 10x20 tray

Spread evenly, press gently, mist surface.

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 4-5 days

Standard protocol - cover, 18-22°C, mist daily.

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** When stems 2-3cm

Bright light for best color development in red/purple varieties.

### 7. Watering

Bottom watering preferred. Keep moist.

### 8. Harvest

**Timing:** 12-16 days, height 5-8cm, good color developed

**Yield:** 150-250g from 10x20 tray

### 9. Post-Harvest

Handle gently to preserve leaves. Refrigerate, 5-7 days shelf life.

## Pro Tips

1. **Heat Tolerant** - Better summer performer than spinach.
2. **Easier Than Amaranth** - Similar color potential with easier growing.
3. **Purple Power** - Purple orach provides dramatic color impact.
4. **Mild Flavor** - Don't oversell flavor - it's subtle. Focus on color and nutrition.
