## Overview

Sorrel microgreens offer a unique tangy, lemony flavor that's unlike anything else in the microgreen world. The sourness comes from oxalic acid, the same compound that gives rhubarb its tang. This distinctive flavor makes sorrel a specialty item that chefs prize.

Sorrel is slow to germinate and grow, making it an advanced crop. The seeds benefit from pre-soaking, and the plants take patience to reach harvestable size. But the unique flavor justifies the effort for growers serving specialty markets.

Red-veined sorrel varieties add visual interest with burgundy veining on green leaves.

## Best Uses

- Accent for fish dishes (lemon substitute)
- Tangy salad component
- High-end restaurant garnish
- Unique flavor profile for adventurous eaters
- French cuisine

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Sorrel (Rumex acetosa) or red-veined sorrel
- Small, brown, triangular seeds
- Fresh seeds for best germination

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location
- Use within 2 years

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** Yes - improves slow germination

**Process:**
1. Soak 8-12 hours in cool water
2. Drain and rinse
3. Plant promptly

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Fine coco coir or soil

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 20-30g per 10x20 tray

Spread evenly, press lightly, don't cover.

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 4-5 days (slow germinator)

Keep at 15-20°C, mist daily, be patient.

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** When germination visible

Moderate light, 12-14 hours daily.

### 7. Watering

Keep moist but not wet. Bottom watering preferred.

### 8. Harvest

**Timing:** 14-21 days, height 4-6cm, distinctive shape visible

**Yield:** 80-150g from 10x20 tray

### 9. Post-Harvest

Handle gently. Refrigerate, 5-7 days shelf life.

## Pro Tips

1. **Unique Flavor** - The tangy/sour flavor has no substitute. Market this uniqueness.
2. **French Connection** - Sorrel is traditional in French cuisine. Target French restaurants.
3. **Fish Perfect** - Natural lemon-like acidity pairs perfectly with fish.
4. **Patience Required** - Slow crop. Plan 3 weeks from seed to harvest.
5. **Red-Veined Variety** - Adds visual interest with burgundy coloring.
