## Overview

Lettuce microgreens offer the familiar, mild lettuce flavor in miniature form. Various lettuce types - romaine, butterhead, oakleaf, lollo rosso, and others - can be grown as microgreens, each with slightly different leaf shapes and colors.

Growing lettuce microgreens requires more attention than brassicas. Lettuce seeds need light to germinate, prefer cooler temperatures, and are prone to bolting (becoming bitter) in warm conditions. The seeds are also tiny and can be challenging to spread evenly.

Mixed lettuce seed blends produce visually interesting trays with varied colors and textures.

## Best Uses

- Salads (naturally!)
- Sandwich topping
- Burger and wrap garnish
- Mild microgreen mixes
- Customers who don't like strong flavors

## Step-by-Step Growing Guide

### 1. Seed Selection & Storage

**What to look for:**
- Lettuce mix or specific variety (romaine, butterhead, etc.)
- Very tiny seeds
- Red varieties add color interest

**Storage:**
- Cool, dry location (refrigeration extends viability)
- Use within 1-2 years for best germination

### 2. Pre-Soaking

**Required:** No - seeds are too tiny and benefit from light

### 3. Tray Preparation

**Medium:** Fine coco coir or seed-starting mix

**Setup:**
1. 1-2cm of very fine, level medium
2. Pre-moisten well
3. Smooth surface critical for tiny seeds

### 4. Seeding

**Density:** 15-25g per 10x20 tray (tiny seeds)

**Technique:**
1. Use shaker for even distribution
2. Mix seeds with fine sand if needed
3. Do NOT cover - lettuce needs light to germinate
4. Mist very gently

### 5. Blackout Phase

**Duration:** 3-4 days (minimal darkness needed)

**Method:**
1. Cover LOOSELY - some light helps lettuce germinate
2. Keep COOL - 15-18°C ideal
3. Mist gently daily

**Note:** Unlike brassicas, lettuce actually benefits from some light during germination.

### 6. Light Phase

**When to move:** When germination begins (often day 2-3)

**Light requirements:**
- Moderate light
- 12-14 hours per day
- Avoid heat buildup

### 7. Watering

**Method:** Very gentle misting or bottom watering

**Frequency:**
- Keep consistently moist
- Lettuce is shallow-rooted
- Don't let dry out, but don't overwater

### 8. Harvest

**Timing indicators:**
- 10-14 days from seeding
- Height: 4-6cm
- Cotyledons fully developed
- Before any bitterness develops

**Yield:** 80-150g from 10x20 tray (lower yield)

### 9. Post-Harvest

**Handling:**
1. Very gentle - lettuce is delicate
2. Minimal washing
3. Dry carefully

**Storage:**
- Refrigerate immediately at 2-4°C
- Shelf life: 5-7 days
- Best used fresh

## Common Problems & Solutions

### Problem 1: No Germination

**Symptoms:** Seeds don't sprout

**Causes:** Seeds buried, too dark, too warm, old seeds

**Solution:** Surface sow, provide some light, cool down

**Prevention:** Never cover seeds, keep cool, fresh seeds

### Problem 2: Bitter Flavor

**Symptoms:** Lettuce tastes bitter

**Causes:** Too warm, water stress, grown too long

**Solution:** Harvest earlier, keep cooler

**Prevention:** Cool growing conditions (under 18°C)

### Problem 3: Leggy/Weak Growth

**Symptoms:** Stretched, thin stems

**Causes:** Insufficient light, too warm

**Solution:** More light, cooler temps

**Prevention:** Adequate light from start

## Pro Tips

1. **Cool is Essential** - Lettuce microgreens need cool conditions. Heat causes bitterness and bolting.

2. **Light for Germination** - Unlike most seeds, lettuce needs light to germinate. Don't bury or blackout completely.

3. **Familiar Flavor** - Everyone knows lettuce. Easy to market despite lower yields.

4. **Color Mixes** - Red and green lettuce varieties together create visual appeal.

5. **Summer Challenge** - May need to pause lettuce in hot weather.

---

## Scheduling Recommendations

- **Continuous supply:** Plant every 5-7 days
- **Seasonal adjustment:** Easier in cool weather
- **Match to demand:** Familiar product, steady demand
